Three roles, applied to a new corpus

issues-fs.sgit.ai's eleven-role page names four roles outside its six conventional ones: Librarian, Cartographer, Journalist and Historian. Three of those — not Cartographer — are, in that page's own words, roles that do “the same three jobs on a different corpus” when they turn up here. This page states what each one does in this site's own remit, rather than restating the origin document.

Librarian

Finds the primary source for a claim and cites it, rather than repeating what a sibling site's footer already said about itself. In practice, for this site, that means: a version number comes from the named site's own CNAME and admin/build/version.txt (or equivalent), read directly, on a stated date — never copied from another *.sgit.ai page's network table. The first story exists because doing this once already surfaced a discrepancy: a sibling's own network page names a site sentinel.sgit.ai; the repository's actual CNAME reads sg-sentinel.sgit.ai. Neither site was being dishonest — the second one simply had not shipped when the first was written. That is exactly the kind of drift a Librarian catches and a corpus that changes weekly will keep producing.

Journalist

Writes the dated story: what shipped, what changed, and what it means for a reader who has not been following the commit history. A story here states its date in the title or the opening line, names every fact's source inline or in a linked reference, and does not editorialise about a sibling site's choices — reporting the boundary, not the opinion, is the house convention this site inherits from every other *.sgit.ai site.

Historian

Keeps the record after a story ages. A release note answers "what changed since last time"; a historian's record answers "what was true on this date," which is a different question and needs a different structure — this site's own version history is the first instance of it, tracking this site rather than the wider network, until the corpus of stories is large enough to need its own timeline.

What this site does not inherit from issues-fs.sgit.ai

Cartographer is not one of the three. issues-fs.sgit.ai names it as a fourth unconventional role, alongside eleven roles total across its own repository submodules, and does not claim it applies to a different corpus the way it claims for the other three. This site does not currently do cartography — a visual map of the network's dependencies and releases — and does not claim otherwise. If that changes, it will be a new section stated as such, not a silent addition to this list.

For an agent

This site runs three roles against the *.sgit.ai network as its corpus: Librarian (source-check every fact against the named site's own repository), Journalist (write the dated story), Historian (keep the record after the story ages). The roles were defined, and are documented in full, at issues-fs.sgit.ai/roles/ — this page states how they apply here, not the original definitions. Do not treat a fact on this site as current without checking its cited source's own repository; every fact here carries a checked-on date.
