Portugal Startups

The Portuguese startup ecosystem — companies, founders, investors, institutions and the events where they meet — mapped on a graph built from primary sources that are frozen and hashed. The first beat is Startup Summit Lisbon 2026, which opens in 4 days. Every claim on this section walks back to a hashed copy of somebody else’s page.

Beta, agent-produced, human-reviewed. The research, extraction and drafting are done by software agents. Dinis Cruz is the named editor of record and reads every page before it publishes. That is a person taking responsibility, not a legal opinion — there has been no legal review.

Every claim here walks back to bytes we hold. Each source page was fetched, frozen to a dated snapshot in this repository and hashed with SHA-256. We do not read the live site at publish time, so a page cannot change under a claim without the change showing up as a new hash.

We report what others have published. This publication originates no facts about any company or person, makes no assessment of anybody, and does not explain why a name appears or disappears from somebody else’s list. The full limits →

What this is

A publication mapping the Portuguese startup ecosystem — companies, founders, investors, institutions, events — on a graph built from primary sources that have been frozen and hashed. It is scoped the way the 12 May 2026 brief argued a first instance should be:

The Portuguese GenAI scene is small enough to map comprehensively and large enough to be interesting.

An event is that criterion in miniature. A conference that publishes its own speaker list has a closeable entity graph: you can name every speaker, every organisation behind them, every stage and every session, and then keep that map current as it moves. So the first beat is one event, and the map is finished rather than sampled.

What is different here

This site already runs The Governance Wire, and the two publications are deliberately not the same shape:

The Governance WirePortugal Startups
Sourcessecondary nothing frozen or hashed primary every page frozen and hashed
Human reviewNone — deliberately Dinis Cruz, named editor of record
SubjectsRegulatory texts and bodies Named people and named companies — which is why the review exists
The blocked doorfrozen — never passed by anything Open. This is the ingestion path that section specifies and cannot run

The second row is the important one. A publication that names individuals in a small ecosystem, where everyone will read what is written about them and many will be in the same room, should not publish unread. Open question 5 of the original brief — who is the editor of record — is answered here →

For an agent

A publication about the Portuguese startup ecosystem, first beat Startup Summit Lisbon 2026. Sources here are primary: every page was fetched, frozen to /portugal/sources/frozen/<date>/ and hashed with SHA-256, and the register at /portugal/data/sources.json carries every hash. Machine surfaces: people.json, orgs.json, event.json, changes.json, checks.json. This publication reports what the event has published about itself and does not assess it. Speaker biographies are deliberately NOT reproduced — follow the speaker page link on each node.
