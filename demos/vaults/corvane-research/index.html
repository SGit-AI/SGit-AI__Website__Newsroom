Research, delivered as a vault: Corvane Research on Tallyfern Pay

Vendor research as a living vault instead of a PDF, on synthetic data.

Everything in this vault is synthetic. Corvane Research, Tallyfern, Harbourline Group and every person, finding, timing and price in it are invented, to show how a company that sells research could deliver it as a vault. None of it describes a real company or product. The article explains the idea.

newsroom.sgit.ai

The SGit Newsroom: one page, one argument, with the figures, the data and the links to check it. Articles by Dinis Cruz, written from his voice notes by an agent working as the newsroom; the roles and the rules they work under are public. The method is published so that anyone can run a newsroom like it. All content CC BY 4.0 unless a page states otherwise.
