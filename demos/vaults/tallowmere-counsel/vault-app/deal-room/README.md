# Tallowmere Counsel: the deal room (made-up demonstration)

A single-page vault app for newsroom.sgit.ai, for the article
https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/legal-on-demand-for-startups.html

**Everything here is made up.** Tallowmere Counsel, Brindlewood Security, Kestrel Row Ventures and every person,
number, term and clause are invented. The legal wording is illustrative, not legal advice. The negotiation
playback is a scripted, deterministic simulation; no model is called.

This is where the two sides meet: Brindlewood Security's founder desk and Kestrel Row Ventures' investor desk,
each a team of agents and a human lawyer, agree a £1.2m seed round between 2 and 7 November 2026.

## What it shows

- **Overview**: an infographic of the two teams (humans plus agents), the deal room in the middle, and the flow:
  tell your team what you want, they hash it out, both sign.
- **The agreement**: the fractal agreement in five levels (intent, principles, terms, clauses, plain English and
  Portuguese projections). Every node has its own SHA-256 hash, every parent hashes its children, and the root is
  signed. You can zoom in and out, see which principles each clause serves, and tamper with any text to watch the
  root and its signatures fail.
- **Versions and diffs**: v1 to v4 with signed roots, the status of each principle per round, and word-by-word
  diffs per node showing who changed what and why.
- **Understandings**: the three signed understandings ("Here is our understanding" and "Yes, that is our
  understanding"), the consistency check of every version, and a replay of the moment v3 was blocked.
- **Signing**: the signing ceremony on the root of v4 (both counsel, both principals, the Tallowmere seal), QR
  codes, a verification panel and a tamper control.
- **Message log**: an append-only log between the desks; each entry is signed by its sender and hashes the one
  before it.
- **Playback**: the five days as a timeline with a virtual clock (play, pause, step, speed).
- **The meter**: every task priced, with a receipt signed by Tallowmere, who pays for what, the plans, and an
  illustrative comparison with a traditional engagement.
- **Keys**: every party, its public key fingerprint and what it has signed.

The signatures are real ECDSA P-256 signatures over canonical JSON. The page verifies each one in the browser
with the Web Crypto API (`crypto.subtle`), and falls back to the results computed at build time if Web Crypto is
not available. Only public keys are included.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app (inline CSS and JS, an inlined MIT-licensed QR code encoder by Kazuhiko Arase, and
  an embedded copy of the data). It makes no network requests other than reading `data.json` next to it.
- `data.json`: everything the app shows, generated by the build script.
- `app.json`: vault app manifest.
- `README.md`: this file.

Companion apps: `../founder-desk/index.html` and `../investor-desk/index.html`.
