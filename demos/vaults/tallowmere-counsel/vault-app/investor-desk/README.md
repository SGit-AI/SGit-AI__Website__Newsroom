# Investor desk (Tallowmere Counsel demonstration)

A made-up demonstration for newsroom.sgit.ai, made for the article
https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/legal-on-demand-for-startups.html.
Tallowmere Counsel, Brindlewood Security, Kestrel Row Ventures and every person, number and term here are invented.
Not legal advice. The clause wording is illustrative.

This app is Kestrel Row Ventures' side of a seed round, working with Tallowmere's investor desk (Maya Kestrel,
partner; Owen Fairley, counsel; ten scripted agents). It has these sections:

- Overview: the team (humans and agents), the agreed deal and the headline numbers.
- Pipeline: Brindlewood among four other invented startups, with hours spent in each stage.
- Diligence: the 14-document data room (with hashes and who prepared each one), 12 red/amber/green findings, and
  12 questions billed to the investor, with the founder side's signed answers.
- Principles: the founder's eight published principles, the desk's position on each and the risk it moves.
- Negotiation: versions v1 to v4 as hash trees you can zoom through, word-by-word diffs, the signed understandings,
  and the consistency check that blocks v3 (clause 7.2) and passes v4.
- IC memo: generated by the Memo agent and signed by the partner, with the cap table arithmetic.
- Desk run: a scripted, deterministic playback (no model is called) with a virtual clock and a running bill.
- Bill and speed: plans, chained Tallowmere-signed receipts, a signed statement and an illustrative comparison
  with a traditional engagement.
- Signatures: every party's public key and fingerprint, what each signed, a tamper lab and QR codes.

Every signature is a real ECDSA P-256 signature over canonical JSON and is verified in the browser with WebCrypto.
Only public keys are included.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app (inline CSS and JS, with a vendored MIT QR encoder, qrcode-generator by Kazuhiko
  Arase). It loads `data.json` and falls back to an embedded copy, so it also works opened as a file.
- `data.json`: everything the app shows, generated by a build script.
- `app.json`: vault app manifest.
- `README.md`: this file.
