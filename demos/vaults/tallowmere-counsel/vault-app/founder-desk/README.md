# Founder desk: Brindlewood Security with Tallowmere Counsel

A made-up demonstration for newsroom.sgit.ai, for the article
https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/legal-on-demand-for-startups.html

Tallowmere Counsel, Brindlewood Security, Kestrel Row Ventures, Wickhatch and every person, number and term here
are invented. The legal wording is illustrative, not legal advice.

This vault app shows the founder's side of a seed round: Ana Brindle with Tallowmere's founder desk (Rui Calder,
counsel, and a team of agents). It covers:

- the founder's published terms (intent and eight principles), signed by the founder, with a QR code to share them;
- readiness across company, shares and cap table, IP (including the Apache-2.0 licence), employment and data
  protection, and the data room it builds, each document hashed and the manifest signed;
- a cap table modeller: before, Kestrel Row's v1 offer, and the agreed deal (new shares plus existing founder shares
  sold, and the loan back), with the arithmetic;
- the mirror check on Kestrel Row's v1 term sheet and what the founder desk proposed in v2;
- plain-language explanations of v1's terms, each with an example in numbers;
- versions v1 to v4 as hash trees with signed roots, word-by-word diffs, signed understandings and the consistency
  check that blocked v3;
- a scripted, deterministic playback of the run (no model is called) with a running bill;
- the bill: Tallowmere-signed receipts, the plan, who pays, and an illustrative comparison with traditional fees;
- a signature panel: every party, its key fingerprint and what it signed.

Every signature is real ECDSA P-256 and is verified live in the browser with WebCrypto. Use the "Tamper" controls
to edit a signed text and watch its verification fail.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app (inline CSS and JS, a vendored MIT QR encoder, no network requests). It loads
  `data.json` and carries an embedded copy as a fallback.
- `data.json`: everything the app shows, including public keys, payloads and signatures. Generated by a build script.
- `app.json`: vault app manifest.
- `README.md`: this file.

Sibling apps: `../investor-desk/index.html` (Kestrel Row's side) and `../deal-room/index.html` (where both sides sign).
