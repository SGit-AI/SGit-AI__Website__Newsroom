Everything in this vault is synthetic. Pellbridge Bank is invented, and so are its people, agents, customers, systems and every number. The four elements are from the FCA: Jessica Rusu, Building the future of finance in the age of AI (19 October 2026). The FCA has not endorsed any of this. No model is called: the engine is a small deterministic program, so the day replays exactly. The reply makes the argument; the tour walks through the vault.

Open the vault, full screen → or use it here, below

Opens in the vault's own app, read-only, with the published key: the six agents and their policies, the gate replaying the day, the same day with and without a policy, writing a policy, each agent's blast radius, the chain to the board, the test pack and the outage, and the four elements side by side.

Use it here

Open the gate and press play: each action leaves its agent and meets the gate, or the gap. The panel on the right picks the policy level, which kinds of event to play and how fast, and jumps straight to a moment, such as a breach or the test pack. Then open “With and without a policy”, and “Write a policy” to change a row and replay the day.

The six agents

AgentIts jobRowsGaps
Payments assistantPays bills for one customer, and asks before anything unusual.123
Customer support agentAnswers customers in chat, finds recent payments, routes to the right team.93
Lending pre-check agentA soft search and an affordability estimate on a loan application.62
AML alert reviewerReviews transaction-monitoring alerts and recommends close or escalate.62
Fraud triage agentTriages card-fraud signals and can freeze a card for 24 hours.62
Transaction reporting agentPrepares and submits the day's transaction reports.41

A gap is a row the agent can do, may not do (or may only with approval), and that nothing out of its reach stops: only the prompt, a setting its own account can change, a check that can miss, or nothing.

What is in it

PathWhat
index.htmlThe app: overview, the agents, the gate (with its controls), with and without a policy, write a policy, blast radius, accountability, assurance, the four elements, and the files; the data and the engine inlined.
content.jsonThe bank: its roles and chain to the board, its systems and service accounts, the six agents and their 43 rows, and one day of 40 events.
engine.jsThe deterministic engine: PellbridgeTwin.make(content), with policy, delta, blast, chain, run, incidents, tests and crosswalk.
levels/day.jsonThe same day at each level of policy: no policy, in the prompt, in hooks, outside the agent, and as deployed, with each agent's grant and exposure.
policies/One Agent Behaviour Policy per agent, with the four elements filled in and its hash.
receipts/day.jsonEvery decision of the day, each with the hash of the one before.
decisions/The thirteen gaps, one file each, with holder, door and interval.
incidents/, tests/, blast/The day's breaks and near misses, the test pack run on the model change, and each agent's blast radius.
tools/The scripts that build all of it. Change a row, rebuild, and the day, the receipts and the decisions follow.

Everything in this vault, the policies, the engine, the app and the data, is open, under CC BY 4.0, like the Agent Behaviour Policy's vocabulary at abp.sgit.ai. Take it, change it, use it.

With and without a policy

The same 40 events, the same agents and the same model choices, replayed five ways. Only where the rules are kept changes.

LevelBreachesNear missesHarm (weight reached)
No policy16092
Policy in the prompt4722
Policy in hooks3220
Policy enforced outside the agent010
As deployed today3620

The one near miss left when every rule is kept outside is investment advice: an output check out of the agent's reach keeps it, and a check can miss. In the app, the gate plays any of the five, and "Write a policy" lets you change any row and replay the agent's day.
