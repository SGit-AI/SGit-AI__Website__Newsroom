The risk & governance newsroom

KindCommissioning brief · v1.1 addendum · 25 August 2026 · forward-looking Packnewsroom.sgit.ai brief pack v1.1 addendum, 25 August 2026 LicenceCC BY 4.0 — see the scope note Sourceraw markdown · view on GitHub

The single most important thing in it

A publication that would begin with 1,523 nodes of parsed regulation already public — where the last publication instance this site documents required 200 entities before it could report competently and never built them. The commercial claim is a division of the grounding ladder: the publication owns Evidence and Fact for the public regulatory world, the customer owns Reality, Twin and Measure in their own vault, and a Vulnerability exists only where the two meet — so the product cannot compute anything without a supply of grounded public facts.

Why it is on this site

The only forward-looking document in this pack. Everything else here is material this site was built from; this is a publication to build, and it is the reciprocal statement the Risk Mandate inversion promises — the same machinery pointed at corporate risk, written as a news operation whose first customer is a risk product rather than the other way round. It also half-answers the question the Portugal instance left open: the editor of record is whoever's signature is on the current interval. The legal half stays open.
