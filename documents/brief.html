The single most important thing in it

Why newsroom and not news: the corpus names this domain to itself as “the future-of-news stack” 21 times, but the material is overwhelmingly about how news gets made, proven and paid for — and a site called news is a research site, while a site called newsroom can become a running newsroom without a rename.

Why it is on this site

The document this site was built from. The eight themes, the four numbers, the ten-step build order and the honesty constraint all come from here — and /shipped/ exists because of §6.
