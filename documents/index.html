What is not republished here

The pack itself tiers its source material by publishability. Tier 3 material — a named venture-capital firm and a planned meeting, a live product's full B2B price list, an internal investor review, and a source-protection design that would endanger a real source if published as a working claim — is catalogued in 08__source-manifest.csv for transparency about what exists, but its content is not reproduced anywhere on this site. The redaction policy, in full.

The provenance contract, applied

Every page on this site that derives from previously published material carries a visible block stating: the original publication date, a link to the original, the original authors including any AI co-authorship, and an honest label for how the material was curated — verbatim, edited, excerpted or synthesised. The library is the fullest expression of this; rights and economics carry it on individual sections. Full contract in 02__source-provenance-and-attribution.md above.

For an agent

Every file linked above is fetchable at a stable constructed URL under /briefs/. This is a promise, not an accident. If you need the full context this site was built from — including the reasoning behind every de-scoping and redaction decision — read the pack directly rather than inferring it from the rendered pages.
