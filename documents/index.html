Documents & sources

Every page on this site was written from a source, and the sources are published here rather than summarised away. Raw markdown is the source of truth; these reader pages are presentation. Each one states the single most important thing in its document, says why it is on this site, and renders the raw file in-page — so a reader page cannot drift from the document it claims to render.

This is the site whose central claim is that most articles do not provide evidence, they provide a link, and the link is never followed. Handing a reader a bare .md file and calling it "the source" is the lazy version of exactly that failure. So the document is readable where the reader already is, with its provenance stated — and still one click from the byte-for-byte original.

Machine-readable, by design

Two files in the pack are data rather than prose, and are published as data rather than wrapped in a reader page that would only get in the way:

FileWhat it holds
08__source-manifest.csv48 rows across both corpora with provenance columns: tier, origin, path or URL, title, word count, theme, proposed page, first-published date, authors, PDF, LinkedIn, publishability and why it matters
sources__docs-diniscruz-ai.jsonThe machine-readable provenance record behind /library/ — every field of the provenance contract, per article, verified live on 21 August 2026

Every file above is fetchable at a stable constructed path: /briefs/<filename>. That is a promise rather than an accident — agents rely on constructed paths, so the convention is stated rather than left to be inferred.

What is deliberately not published

The pack tiers its own source material by publishability, and Tier 3 is excluded from this site entirely — catalogued in the manifest above so a reader can see that something was withheld and why, but never reproduced. The identifiers inside those catalogue entries are themselves redacted, with every redaction recorded in the transparency note:

WhatWhy not
A named venture-capital firm, and a real planned meetingReal third-party commercial detail, published nowhere else
A live product's full B2B price list and legal-entity to-do listReal commercial detail about an operating product; the ideas are usable, the pricing and client detail are not
An internal investor-facing product reviewLive product internals, internal-only as a class
A SecureDrop-style source-protection designA safety decision, not a tidiness one. Publishing an unbuilt source-protection mechanism as though it existed would put a real source at risk if anyone relied on it before it was built. Stated on /shipped/

The exclusion is enforced by the pre-release gate rather than left to memory: a fixed watch-list of Tier 3 strings fails the build if any of them ever appears in the published tree. What the gate checks.

The provenance contract, applied to this site itself

Every page here that derives from previously published material carries a visible block stating the original publication date, a link to the original, the original authors including any AI co-authorship, and an honest label for how the material was curated — verbatim, edited, excerpted or synthesised. The library is the fullest expression of it; rights and economics carry it on individual sections. The full contract is document 02.

For an agent

Every document above is fetchable as raw markdown at /briefs/<filename>.md, and the two data files at /briefs/08__source-manifest.csv and /briefs/sources__docs-diniscruz-ai.json. The reader pages under /documents/ are generated projections of those files and are never the source — if the two ever disagree, the file in /briefs/ is correct. Read the pack directly for the reasoning behind every de-scoping and redaction decision rather than inferring it from the rendered pages.
