## Two things meeting

The data platform publishes Portugal's public data with an API and the provenance of every value ([ten days to a data platform](ten-days-to-a-data-platform.md)). The site factory makes a repository, an address and a site from one commit ([one commit, one site, one owner](one-commit-one-site-one-owner.md)). Put them together, and a site that shows the data in a new way is a request file and a few minutes of an agent team's work.

## IPMA's data, six ways

IPMA data, drawn: Portugal's warnings, forecasts and readings, read live in the browser, in six designs.

At 10:36 this morning I asked for "a site showing the data we have from IPMA". At 10:48, [ipma-data.agenticcells.site](https://ipma-data.agenticcells.site/) was live: IPMA's warnings by area, forecasts and readings, read live in the reader's browser from the data platform, every figure IPMA's with the time it was read, and "not a warning service" at the top. And six designs of the same data: a calm main page, a broadsheet weather page, a chart room with the sea first, a dark night-desk board with a light per source, an almanac on one A4 sheet, and a phone-first glance.

Six ways to read the same data is the point: the data is the same; what people need from it is not.

## A holiday-homes company that does not exist

At 16:09 the factory received a request for a real-estate demo; at 16:13 [Cinco Chaminés](https://algarve-holiday-homes.agenticcells.site/) was live. It says at the top: "Made-up example. Cinco Chaminés is an invented company: the homes, prices, availability and bookings are simulated, and the pictures are AI-generated. The weather, sea, UV and events are real, read live from data.promptugal.news."

Pick your dates and it shows which of five homes from Tavira to Sagres are free, simulated, and what the weather, the sea and the local events look like for your stay, real, from IPMA and the towns' own agendas. A scripted agent answers questions about the stay, and a live page links to beach webcams; it links rather than embeds them, because the camera operators' terms require permission to relay their streams.

This is the intersection I keep talking about. A holiday-homes site is more useful when it tells you what you can do there, what the sea will be like, and what is on that week. Each of those is a source on the platform; each new idea for a site finds the next source to add, cameras next.

## Demos as experiments

The same afternoon the factory made demo sites for weather, for events, and for cyber, the last one showing how the PrompTugal.news team will capture and publish threat intelligence; and, for fun, a London billboard, labelled "100% simulated". Each took minutes.

This changes how I can explore. What would this look like for a real-estate agency? For a hotel? For a council? For an insurer? Each question is a site, published in ten or twenty minutes, labelled as a demo, and shown to the person who might want it. And each is also a demo of the agentic cell itself, a team of agents making a professional site from a brief ([the website cell](the-website-cell.md)).

## What this does not claim

- **Every demo is labelled as a demo;** invented companies are invented.
- **The live data is as fresh as the data platform's last read,** which each page shows.
- **Webcams are linked, not shown,** until their operators allow it.
