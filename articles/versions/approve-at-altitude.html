Versions of: Approve at altitude

1 published version of Approve at altitude: decisions at every level, from strategy to one agent's step, where each level may narrow the one above and never widen it. The current one is v1.0.0.

v1.0.0 is the article as first published. A change to its text (title, abstract or body) is the next minor version, v1.1.0; a change only to its other details (a LinkedIn link, the release it belongs to) is the next patch, v1.0.1. The list is read from the site's git history at every build, so it cannot be edited by hand and cannot leave a version out. Views extracted from an article, such as the Five readers, are not part of its text and do not change its version.

sgit.ai

sgit is git for encrypted vaults, clone, commit, branch and merge files that are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they leave your machine. Open source, Apache-2.0, in beta and powering production workflows.

🔒 Thirty vaults on this site open in your browser with published read keys, every byte decrypted client-side, none of it readable by the server that stores it. The pages that describe them are ordinary static files. The medium is the message.
