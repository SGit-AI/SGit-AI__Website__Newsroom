Versions of: On the record

3 published versions of On the record: signed opinions from experts, paid when readers value them, and a share for the people whose ideas an article uses. The current one is v1.2.0.

v1.0.0 is the article as first published. A change to its text (title, abstract or body) is the next minor version, v1.1.0; a change only to its other details (a LinkedIn link, the release it belongs to) is the next patch, v1.0.1. The list is read from the site's git history at every build, so it cannot be edited by hand and cannot leave a version out. Views extracted from an article, such as the Five readers, are not part of its text and do not change its version.

newsroom.sgit.ai

The SGit Newsroom: one page, one argument, with the figures, the data and the links to check it. Articles by Dinis Cruz, written from his voice notes by an agent working as the newsroom; the roles and the rules they work under are public. The method is published so that anyone can run a newsroom like it. All content CC BY 4.0 unless a page states otherwise.
