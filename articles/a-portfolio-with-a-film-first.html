A portfolio with a film first: Silvio Switha's site, made in under an hour by the site factory and five design agents, with his music video in the first screen, his agents as a solar system and a mixing desk, a producer's studio, a proposal for clients and a gig poster, and every claim something he built

By Dinis Cruz · 2026-11-16 · article v1.0.0 · newsroom v0.14.130 · careersagenticcellssite-factorydesignmusicagentsweb-summitarticle

Abstract: The same evening as Yiheng Wang's site, the same flow made a site for Silvio Switha, and the result could not be more different, because the evidence is different. Silvio is an AI entrepreneur, a musician and a producer, and at Web Summit he did some of the most spectacular work of the ten days: the 3D universe command centre his team ran on, the Gravity visualisation library, the X-Ray, design work, and Know the Reach, RiskMandate's song and music video, made with AI. He is now looking for projects, as a professional and a consultant. So his site is a portfolio, and it leads with the film. The factory took the request to a live HTTPS site in 55 seconds and a first version in just over five minutes; then five design agents built five designs of the same facts in about eleven minutes of commits: Premiere, cinematic, with the film first; Universe, his agents in orbit as six rings; The Studio, a producer's desk with liner notes, a session timeline drawn like a DAW and his agents as a mixing desk; The Proposal, clean case studies and services for clients; and On Tour, a red and black gig poster. Every figure names its source, and every claim is something he built and can show.

In short

  • A portfolio, not a CV: silvio-switha.agenticcells.site, for an AI entrepreneur, musician and producer looking for projects.
  • The film first: Know the Reach, his song and music video, plays in the first screen of every design.
  • Under an hour: the factory in 55 seconds, a first version in five minutes, five designs in eleven minutes of commits.
  • His agents, drawn three ways: a solar system, bars by group, and a mixing desk.
  • For clients: case studies, services to confirm, and a page that tells your AI how to ask about him.
  • Nothing made up: every figure names its source; everything is something he built.

Who he is

Silvio Switha was one of the six of us at Web Summit, and he did some of the most spectacular work of the ten days (ten days at Web Summit). He built, through his dashboard agent, the 3D command centre his team ran on, UNIVERSO, with X-Ray and Gravity beside it. He made the team site's home page designs. And he made Know the Reach, RiskMandate's song and music video, with AI, from about 2,300 words of his direction (know the reach, every hour is twenty years). He has been a musician for twenty years, a singer and a producer, and as I wrote then, AI "changed the speed, not the vision".

He is now a full AI entrepreneur, working with several start-ups and looking for projects, as a professional and a consultant. He agreed to have this site and to be named. So the brief was a portfolio: what he has built, how he works, and how to hire him.

Under an hour

The flow was the one that made Yiheng Wang's site an hour earlier (a website that is her). The request was validated at 20:43:50; the factory created the repository, the DNS record and the custom domain, deployed, and served HTTPS at 20:44:45, 55 seconds later; and Claude Code's first version, written from the brief and his public sources, was pushed at 20:49:04 (the site factory works).

Then a separate session took the site's own instructions, the full film, thirteen of his photos and screenshots of his work, with one direction from me: the video has to be very high on the profile. It built a shared base at 21:23 (the film, the photos, his agents as data, a picker) and five design agents built five designs between 21:29 and 21:34. At 21:36 it wrote the brief for his own conductor, to take the site over, in Portuguese and English.

The film first

Every design shows the film in its first screen: Know the Reach, 3 minutes 38 seconds, music and video by Silvio, with a silent loop playing behind his name and a button to watch it with sound. Then its credits, and then its making, drawn as a funnel from words to film: about 2,300 words of direction in 78 messages, 43 generated images, 16 motion clips, 21 edited exports, one film, and the eight decisions that shaped it. That is the strongest possible opening for someone selling what he makes: not a claim, the thing itself.

Five designs

  • Premiere: cinematic, the film first, then the work, the agent team, how he works and his music. The default.
  • Universe: his own visual language, his agents in orbit around him in six rings, and each piece of work as a world you fly to.
  • The Studio: a producer's desk: liner notes for the film, the session drawn as a DAW arrangement, other productions, his agents as a mixing desk, and a studio wall of photos.
  • The Proposal: for clients: services, three case studies in one format (the brief, what he did, how, the result), his process and his team.
  • On Tour: a gig poster in red and black: the single, the stage, the crew as backstage passes, the rig, twenty years on the road, and a box office.

His agents, drawn three ways

The same data, his 17 agents, 13 of them running, and their commits from his dashboard, is drawn three ways. Universe puts them in orbit, six rings around him, lead, build, creative, growth, operations and safeguards, each moon sized by its commits. Premiere lists the cast as bars by group, each with what it does. And The Studio draws them as a mixing desk: each agent a channel, each fader set to its commit count. The rule under all three is his own line from the summit: agents with a mandate. They draft; he decides and sends.

A producer's studio and a gig poster

The Studio is the design I would show anyone who doubts what design agents can do. The film plays on a monitor beside a single's cover; the credits are liner notes; and the making of the film is drawn as a session in a music editor, with tracks for direction, images, motion, exports and the master, each block one item from the production audit.

On Tour goes the other way: loud, red and black, a poster for his ten days, with the crew as backstage passes, one per agent, and the rig, the tools he built, as flight cases.

The work, and how to hire him

The work is all real, and all linked: the UNIVERSO command centre, with a screen recording of it running; the X-Ray, a live search that maps what a company's AI agents can reach; Gravity, a universe-visualisation library others can reuse, already drawing the Lisbon Tech Fringe; the design lab; the video pipeline. At the summit, every message his outreach agents drafted for fifty target companies was sent by him.

Each design ends with how to hire him, in its own voice: services in The Proposal, a box office on tour, a studio booking. The kinds of project are marked as a proposal for Silvio to confirm, with no prices: a command centre or dashboard for a team, a data visualisation, a product video or music, an agent team for a start-up, design. And like Yiheng's site, it tells a hirer how to point their own AI at it, read-only, answering only from the sources.

It is him

The same point as for Yiheng, and it matters as much. Nothing here is invented. The film is his film, the universe is his universe, the numbers are from the production audit and his public dashboard, each with a marker linking to its source. Teammates are cropped out of the photos, and names were blurred in a screenshot, because they have not agreed to appear. Where something is missing, the site says so: contact details to follow, the band not named until he names it.

So when a founder asks him how he made the film, how the universe works, or how he ran outreach with agents, he can show them, because he did it. And the site, like Yiheng's, is now his to steer with his own agent.

Two sites, one evening

Two sites in one evening, from the same factory, the same guidance and the same design-agent method, and they look nothing alike, because the evidence was different: a project manager's brigade, a producer's studio. That is what I mean when I say the agents have learned our way of working: they read the guidance and the sources, they stay within their policies, and they are free to be creative with what is really there (show me the evidence).

What this does not claim

  • A first version: made with his permission; Silvio approves every change about him, and his own agent takes it from here.
  • The timings are from the factory's status file and the site's commits; the design time is the span of the design commits.
  • The services are a proposal for him to confirm, as the site says.
  • Screenshots from the evening of 16 November; the site will change.
