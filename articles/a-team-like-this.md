## What built it

Behind everything in this series is a team of agents, steered by a few people ([ten days to a data platform](ten-days-to-a-data-platform.md)):

- **Conductors** that take a brief and hand out the work, one per account.
- **A place agent for every town,** keeping each place's file and its sources.
- **Data agents** that collect the public sources, as code, and keep the register.
- **A security agent** that reviews every change before and after, sweeps every repository hourly, and keeps a public log.
- **A behaviour-policy agent** that writes and tightens a policy for every agent, 72 of them.
- **A librarian** with its own team, and **outreach** that drafts every request for a person to send.
- **Designers and developers** that build each new site.

## What we sell

The code is open source and the data is public, so the platform is not what we sell: anyone can take it ([governance from zero](governance-from-zero.md)). What we sell is a team like this one, set up for a customer, with its roles, its policies and its security passes, running their sources, their sites and their workflows. AgentiCCells already offers it, and shows our own team as the example: "what we are going to do here is exactly what we want to sell" ([our own team, run as a cell](https://agenticcells.ai/our-team-as-a-cell/)). The smallest version is a website cell; the largest is a network of teams like PrompTugal.news's ([the website cell](the-website-cell.md)).

And it is the most practical demonstration of agent behaviour policies I know: dozens of agents, moving fast, each with a written policy, every change reviewed, and a person who can stop it all ([what can your agent reach?](what-can-your-agent-reach.md)).

## Who I want to talk to

Who to talk to, and what each could do with the platform: use it, integrate, fund it, or feed it.

PrompTugal.news is no longer a small publication; it is a data set about places, with its sources and licences, that grows every day. What it needs now is use: people trying it, integrating with it, and paying for what it does for them. The people I want to talk to:

- **Councils and regional bodies:** their own data, published openly, and a picture of their place that joins it to everyone else's.
- **Tourism and hospitality:** what is on, the weather and the sea, for visitors, the way the holiday-homes demo shows ([sites on the data](sites-on-the-data.md)).
- **Universities and research centres:** a platform for their data and their students, like the heritage centre in the Algarve ([HeritageTech, together](heritage-tech-together.md)).
- **Newsrooms and their associations:** stories with their evidence, and money back to the sources ([paid by the read](paid-by-the-read.md)).
- **The platforms we cannot read yet:** to show their public data with credit, and pay them for it ([whose event is it?](whose-event-is-it.md)).
- **Librarians,** and the people who fund them ([the librarian](the-librarian.md)).
- **Anyone who needs to know what is happening in a place,** insurers, estate agents, civil protection, investors.

Real integrations bring real traffic, and real traffic brings real budget and revenue. That is the next stage.

## What this does not claim

- **No customer has bought a team like this yet;** our own team is the example.
- **The list of people to talk to is a plan;** nobody on it has been approached for this.
- **Prices are on agenticcells.ai,** most of them first proposals.
