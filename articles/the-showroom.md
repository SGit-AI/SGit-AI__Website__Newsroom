## The showroom

The hub at [hub.sovereignweb.site](https://hub.sovereignweb.site/) says it in one line: "Websites their owners own." Below it: "Built by agent teams, then handed over: the owner keeps the files, the history and the address. Every site here is public and live; open one to see it working." When I read it on 21 November it counted 33 sites working, 11 people's sites and one handed over, in seven groups: people, startups, providers, businesses, PrompTugal.news, demos and tools. Each entry is a record in the hub's own data, with the site's address, its repository and its status.

## People

People's sites on the hub, each made by the site factory from that person's own material.

The people's sites are the heart of it. An actor-musician with her cast list and reels ([Emily Cruz](https://emily-cruz.artist.sovereignweb.site/)). An actor-singer and soprano ([Grace Farrell](https://grace-farrell.artist.sovereignweb.site/)). A creative director with 25 years of wine and spirits packaging ([Sarah Cruz](https://sarah-cruz.designer.sovereignweb.site/)). A founder, the first to take hers over ([the first round trip](the-first-round-trip.md)). A musician and producer with his film ([Silvio Switha](https://silvio-switha.agenticcells.site/)). A project manager who ran a team of agents at Web Summit ([a website that is her](a-website-that-is-her.md)). A singer-songwriter on the Algarve coast, an enterprise AI leader in London, an AI automation expert in the south of Portugal.

They look nothing alike, which is the point: each was made from that person's own material, in their own words, with their own photos credited. And several come in five designs of the same facts, so the person chooses rather than accepts.

## Startups, a provider and tools

Brochures for our startups, a provider guide for Mistral, and tools on the owner's own key.

Five sites are independent brochures, each made only from that company's own public pages, with every claim sourced and dated: RiskMandate ("What it can reach. What you allowed. The gap."), AgentiCCells ("One cell. One job. Your stop button."), SGit, The Cyber Boardroom and Sovereign Website itself. They show a company what its own site could be, built from what it has already published.

Then the sites that do something. The [Mistral provider guide](https://mistral.provider.sovereignweb.site/), built overnight with 33 demos ([one night on Mistral](one-night-on-mistral.md)). A [whois tool](https://whois.tool.sovereignweb.site/) that asks the registries' own records from the browser, with no ads and no account. And [Google Maps on the owner's own key](https://google-maps.sovereignwebsite.site/): a sovereign site can use a paid service, as long as the key and the budget are the owner's ([every tool its own website](every-tool-its-own-website.md)).

## Businesses, news and demos

Made-up businesses, the PrompTugal news family, a simulated billboard, and the hub itself.

Two made-up businesses show what a small company would get: five holiday homes with availability and a simulated booking, and an orange grower with a zone map and an order flow, both saying plainly that the prices are invented and nothing is sent. The PrompTugal family, weather, travel, startups, civic data and the data site behind them, shows the same pipeline making news sites from public data. And the demos explain the machine itself, from [the pipeline that turns one request into a site](https://pipeline-demo.demo.sovereignweb.site/) to a simulated London billboard driven by the weather ([the website cell](the-website-cell.md)).

## The theme that is emerging

Look across them and you can see a theme coming together, and how powerful it is to do this many times. The sites share the same bones:

- **Their own address and their own public repository,** with the full history of every change.
- **Several designs of the same facts,** and a chooser on every page.
- **A start page** for the owner, and a **"use with your AI" page,** so their own Claude or ChatGPT can work on the site.
- **A "how it was made" page,** and sources with the date each was read.
- **Photo credits and plain disclaimers:** independent, not endorsed, fictional where it is fictional.
- **No cookies, no tracking,** and static files any host can serve ([what is a sovereign website](what-is-a-sovereign-website.md)).

None of that was designed up front as a template. It came from making site after site, through one pipeline and the same agents, a design agent that now improves every site on a schedule, and a handover that taught us what the start page must say. Each site makes the next one better. The pattern is becoming the product, and the hub is where anyone can see it working ([the way in](the-way-in.md)).

## What this does not claim

- **The counts are the hub's own,** read on 21 November 2026; they change as sites are added.
- **Most sites have not been handed over yet;** one has.
- **The businesses and the billboard are made up or simulated,** as their own pages say.
