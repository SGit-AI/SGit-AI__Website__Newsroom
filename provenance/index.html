Provenance is the product

Traditional newsrooms have an editorial process readers never see — who tipped the story, who reported it, who checked it, what it cost, what changed between drafts. A newsroom that shows its work earns trust that a black-box newsroom cannot. The differentiator is not the byline; it is the walkable chain.

Every claim links to evidence

Most articles do not provide evidence, they provide a link. The link is never followed, and it could go to a site that no longer exists. There is no reference of what references what. Provenance-as-product means every claim carries a citation the reader can actually follow, and the review process that produced the article is itself a public, walkable artefact — a decision graph with named ownership at every step, not a black box that emits a byline.
