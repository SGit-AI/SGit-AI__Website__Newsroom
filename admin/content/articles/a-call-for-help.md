## In short {#in-short}

- **This is a call for help.** For journalists, editors, students and teachers.
- **Journalists are in an identity crisis.** They love the work; the industry is not helping them through the change.
- **We are short of humans.** The PHP team has 21 members, and two of them are people.
- **Every role should have a human.** Coordinating it, steering its agents, reviewing their work.
- **We cannot pay yet.** We can offer coverage, exposure, credit, and the learning.
- **If this makes money, the people in the roles get paid.**

## Why journalists, and why now {#why}

Journalists clearly have an identity crisis at the moment. They love what they do, but the industry is not really helping them, and it is going through a massive transformation. Many of them have not yet seen agentic workflows doing real work, and so they cannot see where they would add value to them.

There is a win-win here. They need to be exposed to these workflows. We need people. As I argued in [own the stack, and the trust](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/own-the-stack-and-the-trust.html), what is missing from our newsrooms is not more agents. It is more journalists, and more readers.

## Every role should have a human {#roles}

The [PHP story site](https://php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/) has shown that we can create roles: a conductor, collectors for primary sources, agencies and researchers, a researcher that runs the source watch, a librarian, a journalist, a historian, a verifier, a corrections liaison, a sherpa that walks with the reader, and more. Its [team page](https://php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/team/index.html) lists 21 members, and its status data counts 23 agents. Two members are people: me, as the editor, and a project manager.

I would argue that every single one of those roles should have a human connected to it. Not to do the agents' work, but to coordinate the role, steer the agents around it, review what they produce, and decide. The historian's map of [who creates the original](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/who-creates-the-original.html) needs a person who knows which pivot points matter. The corrections liaison has eleven requests drafted and waiting for a person to send them. And the interviews and op-eds of that article are work only a person can do.

As I wrote in [jobs that never scaled](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/jobs-that-never-scaled.html), agents can go in all directions, and you need somebody who provides the direction. That is a person's job, in every role.

## What we ask, and what we offer {#offer}

!shot wn-offer.webp | images/ | A call for help: what we ask, to take a role, review and steer its agents, and do what only a person can, or a few minutes an ask as a power user; what we offer now, no pay yet, but coverage, exposure, credit under your own name, and a close look at a newsroom run with agents; and when there is revenue, the people in the roles are the ones who get paid. | concept

**What we ask.** Take a role, or part of one. Read what its agents produce, steer them, and do what only a person can: the interview, the judgement, the caveat that matters. Or start smaller, as one of the site's [power users](https://php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/collaborators/index.html): a few minutes an ask, trying what is new and saying what works.

**What we offer.** I cannot pay right now. What I can give is a huge amount of coverage and exposure: your name on your work, and credit on the site. And probably the best learning anyone can have on this right now: a front-row seat on how a team of agents, steered by people, runs a newsroom in public, with its briefs, board, releases and mistakes all on the record.

**When there is revenue.** I would pay them if I had the money, and that is the plan. The aim is a model where readers and publishers pay, and if this generates revenue, the people in the roles are the ones who should be paid.

## Who this is for {#who}

- **Journalists and editors,** working or between jobs, who want to see where they add value to agentic workflows.
- **Students,** of journalism or of computing, who want real work on a live story.
- **Teachers,** who want a better sense of what these workflows are, for their own courses.
- **Anyone** who wants to learn more about agentic workflows by working inside one.

## How to get in touch {#contact}

Ask me directly, on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/diniscruz/), or through the [join form](https://php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/collaborators/index.html#join) on the PHP site. I will also be asking people in my own contacts whether they know someone who would be interested. Fundamentally, this is me saying: I have a project, I need people, and I think it would be good for them too.

## What this does not claim {#limits}

- **There is no paid role today.** The offer is credit, exposure and learning.
- **The roles are the PHP site's,** as its team page lists them on 25 October 2026; they will change with the split into six repositories.
- **Nobody has been asked yet.** This is the call.
