## In short {#in-short}

- **Two weeks:** from a Web Summit newsroom to a data intelligence platform for Portugal's places.
- **Twelve data sources** collected by code; **92 sources** on a public register, read or not, and why.
- **A dozen sites** on PrompTugal.news's own domain, six of them made today.
- **The factory** turns one commit into a site, an address and its owner's access.
- **Money back to the sources,** in the open, simulated for now.
- **We are still gaining speed.**

## Why a series {#why}

When something speeds up like this, one article cannot hold it. The repository behind PrompTugal.news has 6,790 commits since its first, at 00:08 on 4 November. The sequence matters more than the size: each step made the next one cheaper, and the last few days have been the fastest. So I am telling it in parts, grouped in three sections.

## What this does not claim {#limits}

- **Most of it is days old;** some sites are first versions or placeholders, and say so.
- **The payout is simulated;** no money moves yet.
- **Counts are as of 18 November 2026,** from the repositories and the live sites.
