## In short {#in-short}

- **Four layers now:** SGit, RiskMandate, AgentiCCells, and Sovereign Website on top.
- **Sovereign Website is our first mass-market product.**
- **Six messages** to test, from "Built by agents, owned by you" to "Where is your website, from 0 to 5?".
- **Ten pages,** with the sovereignty maturity model at the centre.
- **The site is built as a sovereign website itself.**
- **Five homepage designs** to choose from.

## A new layer on the stack {#stack}

Everything we have built this autumn now stacks up, and it helps to see it in one picture:

- **SGit** is the base: encrypted, versioned vaults as an open standard, like git, where work and its history live ([sgit is a standard](/articles/sgit-is-a-standard.html)).
- **RiskMandate** is the governance: behaviour policies that say what agents may and may not do, and the gap between what they can reach and what they were asked to do ([feature behaviour policies](/articles/feature-behaviour-policies.html)).
- **AgentiCCells** sells agentic teams, cells, installed for a customer, each with a job, a deliverable and a behaviour policy ([the cell is the unit](/articles/the-cell-is-the-unit.html)).
- **Sovereign Website** is the new layer on top: a website built by agents and owned by the person it is for ([Sovereign Agentic Website](/articles/sovereign-agentic-website.html)).

Each layer is open and builds on the one below. What changes with the top one is the market. Sovereign Website is the first thing we make that a florist, a bakery or a surf school can buy without knowing the other three exist. That makes it our first mass-market product, and its site has to work for them first.

## Six ways to say it {#messages}

We should test these, not pick one on instinct:

1. **"Your website. Built by agents, owned by you."** The plain promise, for a small business.
2. **"Where is your website, from 0 to 5?"** The maturity model as a question; it invites a check.
3. **"A website under your own roof."** The European message: your files, your domain, your host.
4. **"Five designs. Pick one. It is yours."** Show, then own; it leads with design.
5. **"Stop renting your website."** The challenge to the platforms, from [the pitch](/articles/the-pitch.html).
6. **"Websites built by agents and owned by the people they are for."** For the people who will use and build on the open pattern.

## The ten pages {#pages}

!shot swd-pages.webp | images/ | The ten pages proposed for the first version of sovereignwebsite.ai. | concept

The first version needs ten pages: a home page with one promise and one action; how it works, from the interview to five designs, the handover, changing it by talking and moving it; the maturity model, with a self-check; examples, real sites each shown with its level; prices; moving your site from Wix, Squarespace or WordPress; owning your domain ([your own registrar](/articles/your-own-registrar.html)); the open pattern; partners; and trust, which shows the behaviour policy and the signed commits behind the site.

## The maturity model at the centre {#model}

The idea I like most for the site is the sovereignty maturity model, and it should sit at its centre. It has six levels. **0, rented:** a hosted builder decides everything. **1, exportable:** your words leave, the design stays. **2, a part you do not control:** open software held by one paid plugin, theme or agency component. **3, open, on one host:** open code tied to one host's features. **4, portable:** plain files, open tools, your domain. **5, sovereign and proven:** portable, and deployed on more than one host.

On the site it becomes a one-minute self-check: five questions about where your domain is registered, whether you can export your site, what it depends on, and where it runs, and an answer from 0 to 5 with what it would take to move up. It gives a visitor a reason to engage before they are asked to buy, and it explains the product better than any feature list.

## How to build it {#build}

The site has to be what it sells. That means:

- **A sovereign website itself:** a repository in the SovereignWebsite organisation on GitHub, plain files and open tools, served on GitHub Pages at sovereignwebsite.ai first, with no proprietary part in the middle.
- **Built the way the product is built:** a design agent makes five complete directions, the team picks one, and a content agent writes every page from the articles we have already published.
- **In several languages from the start,** as agenticcells.ai already is, with English and Portuguese first.
- **Every claim true of the site itself:** if a page says your site can move, this site moves; once the pipeline that deploys one commit to many hosts exists, this site is its first proof.
- **A behaviour policy for the team that builds it,** and every commit signed, as this newsroom's now are.

The full pipeline will not be wired on day one, and it does not need to be. The team can work end to end by hand first, and I will wire the flow once the steps are proven.

## What I am asking the team {#ask}

Set up a team to build sovereignwebsite.ai from this brief: a conductor, a designer, a writer, a developer, and a reviewer for security and for the claims. Start from the ten pages, the six messages and the five directions; propose back what is missing; and build it end to end, by hand where the pipeline is not ready. Every site we make should look very good, and this one most of all, because it is the one that sells the rest.

## What this does not claim {#limits}

- **The designs are sketches** drawn for this brief, with placeholder colours and illustrative prices.
- **The site, the self-check and the every-host pipeline do not exist yet.**
- **The prices are the website cell's as published;** the launch offer is a proposal.
