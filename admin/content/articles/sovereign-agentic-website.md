## In short {#in-short}

- **The name:** Sovereign Agentic Website, SAW, at sovereignwebsite.ai.
- **The promise:** built by agents, owned by you.
- **Sovereign:** the files, schema, history and address are yours, and you can move them anywhere.
- **Agentic:** a team of agents builds it, and your own AI changes it, within a behaviour policy.
- **Open:** a pattern anyone can use without paying us, like WordPress; we also host it.
- **AgentiCCells stays the company,** selling agentic teams; this is one of its cells.

## Why a new name {#why}

In the last few weeks we have been making websites for people we know, and in [the website cell](/articles/the-website-cell.html) I described the offer: a repository fully set up, a live site at its own address, five designs by a team of agents, and then the site handed over, for its owner to change with their own Claude. The [site factory](/articles/the-site-factory-works.html) now makes the repository and the live site from a single commit.

The next step was [the sovereign website](/articles/the-sovereign-website.html), a brief to put sovereignty at the top of agenticcells.ai. But writing it, I realised that changing AgentiCCells' site to be about websites would lose something. AgentiCCells is about selling agentic teams, and the website is one example of what such a team can do, a very focused one. If we are going to compete head on with WordPress, Wix and Squarespace, and I think we should, the website needs a brand of its own.

## Why these two words {#words}

**Sovereign,** because it is the claim only we can make among the website builders. In [sovereignty you can move](/articles/sovereignty-you-can-move.html) I argued that sovereignty is not where your data is hosted but whether you can move it: your site, its data and its schema, to anywhere else, on your own terms. The platforms cannot offer that by their own terms of service ([no lock-in](/articles/no-lock-in.html)). And it is where Europe is heading.

**Agentic,** because that is what is different. In 2000 the open web gave you software to run yourself; WordPress made publishing free, but you or someone you paid still did the work. In 2026 a team of agents does the work: it interviews you, builds the site, shows you designs, and afterwards your own AI changes it when you ask. The agents work inside a behaviour policy that says what they may and may not touch, so the site stays yours while they work on it.

## An open pattern, and a hosted version {#open}

!shot saw-open.webp | images/ | A mock-up of the open pattern's page: a repository you own, used free or hosted by us. | screenshot

A sovereign agentic website is a pattern before it is a product. It is a repository the owner controls, holding the site as plain files, its content and its schema, the instructions for the agents and the behaviour policy that limits them, with checks and a deploy that run on any host. Anyone can use that pattern without paying us, the way anyone can install WordPress. That is the open source play, and it is the point: a site you own should not depend on us existing.

We also offer it hosted. The factory sets up the repository and the site, the agents build it, and it is served at its own address on sovereignwebsite.site until the owner points their own domain at it. The prices follow the website cell's: a £500 anchor, and the launch offer of £50 or €50 I proposed in [the sovereign website](/articles/the-sovereign-website.html).

## What we have, as of today {#today}

- **The domains:** sovereignwebsite.ai for the brand, sovereignwebsite.com pointing to it, and sovereignwebsite.site for hosted sites, kept apart from the brand domain as GitHub keeps github.io apart from github.com.
- **The organisation:** github.com/SovereignWebsite, which will hold the open pattern.
- **The factory and the cell,** which already make these sites; what changes is the name they go to market under.

The homepage mock-ups above are two directions, not a design decision: one written for a small business, one leading with the open pattern for the people who will build on it.

## What happens to AgentiCCells {#acc}

Nothing is lost. AgentiCCells remains the company and its site remains about agentic teams, of which the website is one; the [email agentic cell](/articles/the-email-agentic-cell.html) is another. Sovereign Agentic Website becomes the name of one of those cells, the one with the widest market, with its own site and its own story. The partner programme, the example sites and the [registrar advice](/articles/your-own-registrar.html) for owners moving their names all carry across.

## What this does not claim {#limits}

- **The mock-ups are proposals;** the pattern's repository and the hosted service under the new name do not exist yet.
- **Prices are the website cell's,** as published; the £50 launch offer is a proposal.
- **Sovereign, here, means owning and being able to move,** as defined in [sovereignty you can move](/articles/sovereignty-you-can-move.html); it is not a legal or certification claim.
