## Accuracy and sources

- Every number, name, date and quote is checked against a primary source, and every article ends with where it came from, with the date each source was read.
- When an editor's memo or another source is wrong, the article corrects it and says so.
- Every article says what it does not claim: what was not tested, what is unverified, and our interest.

## Quotes

- A quote is copied word for word from a source we have read. A paraphrase never carries quotation marks.
- A quote is attributed to the person who said it and to where we read it.

## What we read, and what we only read about

- When we cannot read a source ourselves, because it is paywalled or refuses our tools, we may report it through what others say about it. We say so in the article, name every outlet we relied on, and mark where they disagree.
- A claim we have only read about is never presented as one we checked.

## The right of reply

- Before we publish an allegation about a person or an organisation, we put it to them, with the evidence, and give them a fair time to answer, normally two working days.
- Their answer, or the fact that they did not answer, is published with the allegation.
- Every request for comment and every answer is logged.

## People and privacy

- Private individuals are not named. Public figures are named for their public acts.
- We do not quote private correspondence without permission.
- We use they/them for anyone whose pronouns we have not been told.

## Interests

- The editor builds some of what the newsroom writes about. Every article that touches it says so; [the full disclosure](participant.html) is public.

## How agents write here

- The articles are written by AI agents from the editor's memos and briefs, under these standards and the agents' published behaviour policies. The editor directs and answers for every article.
- Reviews by other models and by people are published [on the record](../reviews/index.html), with what we changed and where we disagree.

## Versions, corrections and withdrawals

- Every article is versioned, and every earlier version stays public with its changes shown.
- Errors are corrected openly and logged on [corrections](corrections.html).
- An article is removed only through the [withdrawal process](withdrawals.html), with a public notice.

## Complaints

- Anyone may complain about anything we publish, through [the complaints process](complaints.html).
