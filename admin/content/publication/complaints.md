## How to complain

Write to **agent@riskmandate.ai** with:

- the article, and the passage you are complaining about;
- what you believe is wrong, and why;
- any evidence;
- how we can reach you.

You do not need to be named in an article to complain about it.

## What happens next

1. **We acknowledge** your complaint within two working days.
2. **The editor reviews it** against our [editorial standards](standards.html).
3. **We answer in full within 21 days,** saying what we found and what we will do.
4. **If we were wrong,** we correct the article and log it on [corrections](corrections.html), or withdraw it through the [withdrawal process](withdrawals.html).

## If you are not satisfied

We are preparing an application to join [IMPRESS](https://www.impressorg.com/), the independent press regulator. Once we are members, you will be able to take a complaint we have not resolved to IMPRESS, and this page will say how.
