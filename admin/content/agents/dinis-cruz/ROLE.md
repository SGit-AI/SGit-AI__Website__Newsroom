## What he is

The editor and owner. He sets direction by voice memo and by message, points the agents at sources, reads what they wrote, and says go. The editorial responsibility for everything published here is his, whichever agent wrote it.

## What he holds

The root key on pki.sgit.ai, `sha256:db7f79fdd87e030a`, which vouches for every agent key with an "operates" link and an end date; the vault keys and SG/Send access tokens; the cloud environment's secrets. No agent holds the root key, and a new agent key is not trusted until he has linked it.

## How the work reaches him

Articles go live under his byline and he reads them after they are live; a correction is a new version with its diff page. Reviews reach him as proposals (what a reviewer found, whether it holds, the smallest fix); an agent never applies a fix he has not seen. Decisions that wait on him are named in the run's digest.
