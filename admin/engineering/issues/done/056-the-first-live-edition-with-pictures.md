# The first live edition with pictures: the key in the routine's environment, the images and the ledger on the pages

## Outcome
Shipped in v0.12.59, the evening the owner handed the key (14 October) and the morning after. The first live editions: the four wires' editions of 13 October and the Industry Wire's of 15 October, a hero and twelve to fourteen cards each under the two-dollar cap; 71 images for $10.24 in all, OpenRouter's own figures, on what the wires cost (wire/costs.html). What the run taught (square images, Nano Banana rate-limited upstream, Flash misspelling headlines, Pro exact at fourteen cents, the burst and the caps) is in the design note. The routine's step is card 060, on the key reaching its environment.
