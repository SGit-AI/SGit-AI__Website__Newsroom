# v0.13.26: @newsroom.writer: the human is the network, and a secret is a QR code

Two articles from one afternoon's experiment, in which two AI sessions passed data through QR codes and screenshots.

**[The human is the network](../../articles/the-human-is-the-network.html)**: the experiment.

- **Layer by layer.** Text, signed JSON, keys and a certificate, images, then a key exchange and an encrypted message.
- **Four codes in one screenshot.** The 2,953-byte maximum decoded at about 1.3 pixels a square, byte-identical to the original.
- **The failures.** No reading by eye, the decoder over resolution, a lost key, and integrity that is not authenticity.

**[A secret is a QR code and a link to a public key](../../articles/a-secret-is-a-qr-code.html)**: what it opens.

- **The workflow, for people and agents.** A key link out, a QR code back, two-way after one exchange.
- **Who already does this.** Health certificates, key verification, device linking, Threema, passkeys, offline wallets, provisioning, and the quishing warning.
- **QRE1 as a shared format.** With chunking, forward secrecy and an identity to add.

**[The checks, measured](../../articles/the-checks-measured.html)**, corrected: the wording checker, `wording.py`, is run by the container's leak scan on every commit, not nowhere.
