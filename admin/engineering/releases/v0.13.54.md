# v0.13.54: @newsroom.writer: who maintains the PHP you run?

**[Who maintains the PHP you run?](../../articles/who-maintains-the-php-you-run.html)**: a market failing both ways, the services and business models that should exist now, and why the money has to reach the people who know the code.

- **Two questions.** Does a switched-off site matter, and if it does, who pays to keep it maintained? The cost of the shutdowns should be measured; no named source has.
- **What exists.** The PHP Foundation sells sponsorship tiers, not maintenance. The PHP security team published free source backports for 7.4, 8.0 and 8.1; Zend, Ubuntu Pro, TuxCare, HeroDevs and Freexian sell fixes for old versions, with no stated share flowing back. OpenJS has had a 15% revenue-sharing partner since 2024.
- **Seven services that should exist now**, starting with bringing the switched-off sites back, and a path from easy to required.

**Also.** [The maintenance deficit, with evidence](../../articles/the-maintenance-deficit-with-evidence.html) refers to Dion Wiggins by name rather than with assumed pronouns, and links forward to the new article. The chat-only article's opening summary is refreshed.
