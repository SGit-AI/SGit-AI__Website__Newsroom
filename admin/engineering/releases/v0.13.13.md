# v0.13.13: @newsroom.writer: credit now, validated later

[The user keeps the meter](../../articles/the-user-keeps-the-meter.html) is updated from a second voice memo:

- **Credit now, validated later.** The user's browser adds credit as soon as they come back from Stripe; the admin side, which can be offline, later signs a validation, or marks the payment as not accepted.
- **Three states of credit.** Unvalidated, validated, and not valid, which would mean an attack or a bug. The product keeps working on unvalidated credit, because on the client it costs us nothing.
- **Who are we defending against?** Users gaming the client is unlikely in the short term, and easy to detect.
- **A vault shared by two parties.** Pin its first commit, check every later one, and know who wrote what.
- **Where it lives.** user.sgit.ai, or billing.sgit.ai so others can use it easily.
- **Both diagrams redrawn** to show credit added by the user and validated by the admin.
