# v0.13.21: @newsroom.writer: connect your dots backwards

**[Connect your dots backwards](../../articles/connect-your-dots-backwards.html)**: a proposal, and a first prompt, for founders to let their LLM interview them about their path.

- **Why now.** Most paths are not linear, and the success stories we see were often tidied up.
- **The look-back interview.** Seven stages: timeline, side roads, doors, lessons, connections, evidence, and where a startup may be hiding.
- **The doors.** One-way and two-way, as they felt and as they were.
- **Memory and impostor syndrome.** What you can evidence, and an answer made of facts.
- **The prompt.** A first version to paste into Claude or ChatGPT, and the proposal for a toolkit beside the startups finder.
- **Three infographics.** My dots connected backwards, the doors, and the interview.
