# v0.12.97: @newsroom.writer: agent decision policies, a series in four parts

Four new articles, joined by the new trail `decision-policy`:

1. [Three policies for an agent](../../articles/three-policies-for-an-agent.html): behaviour, decision and workflow, where they meet, zooming into one ABP cell, and four states for every element: defined, implemented, enforced, observed.
2. [Approve at altitude](../../articles/approve-at-altitude.html): decisions at every altitude, signed off once and inherited below; a lower level may narrow the one above and never widen it, checked against declared envelopes.
3. [Case law for agents](../../articles/case-law-for-agents.html): the shape of precedent in England and Wales applied to agents, the decision record as a law report, and how a decision earns its way into code.
4. [Three decisions, mapped](../../articles/three-decisions-mapped.html): memory deletion in the SGit adapter for Ruflo, this newsroom's release, and an illustrative refund, element by element.

The legal and governance facts were checked against primary sources: the UNISON judgment, the 1966 Practice Statement as described in Knauer, Pepper v Hart as restated in R (O), the UK Corporate Governance Code's Principle O, ISO Guide 73, OMG's DMN page and Sutton and Barto. Four figures use the new dn- prefix.
