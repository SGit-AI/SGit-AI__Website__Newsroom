# v0.13.32: @newsroom.writer: the controls fitted to the context

**[An analyst's blueprint for RiskMandate](../../articles/an-analysts-blueprint-for-riskmandate.html)**, updated with our positioning.

- **The controls fitted to the context.** A line in the prompt, a hook, a gateway, or monitoring and a person, depending on what is at stake; the behaviour policy says which.
- **More players is good news.** Each vendor has gravity around its own tooling; our remit runs across them.
- **Who plays open source.** The enforcement points often are open, the governance platforms mostly not; we open the policy layer.
- **The more mature, the less we are needed?** Still a lot of value to add, and a mouldable platform that needs use cases.
