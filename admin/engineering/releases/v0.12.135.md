# v0.12.135: @newsroom.writer: no backhanded compliments

Dinis pointed out that "That is a good habit, and I wish more people did it" is a dig at the reader: it praises one person by criticising everyone else, and assumes the others are wrong when they may simply not need it. Removed there, in [governs, enforces, attests](../../articles/governs-enforces-attests.html), and in the two other places the site did the same: "better than most" in [the policy guards the door](../../articles/the-policy-guards-the-door.html), and "miles ahead of most people with ideas" in [can you sell it?](../../articles/can-you-sell-it.html) and its summary. The articles' README gains a voice rule, **No backhanded compliments**: say what the person did, and why it helped.
