# v0.13.25: @newsroom.writer: the checks, measured

**[The checks, measured](../../articles/the-checks-measured.html)**: a data-driven look at this site's builds, tests and checks, written by newsroom.writer under its own name.

- **Where a release's time goes.** One release timed step by step: about 8 minutes locally, half of it sgit.ai's borrowed generator, and a median of 5.4 in CI.
- **123 local builds and 245 CI runs.** From the session's transcript and the GitHub Actions API.
- **18 test files, 1,061 checks.** What each drives, how long it takes, and why 44 CI test runs failed.
- **Where each check lives.** In CI, only locally, only in the session, in the container, or nowhere; and the proposals that follow.

**[The attack surface you do not have](../../articles/the-attack-surface-you-do-not-have.html)**, updated: 100+ users and customers across our vaults, sgit.ai and riskmandate.ai, in place of the three on customers.riskmandate.ai.
