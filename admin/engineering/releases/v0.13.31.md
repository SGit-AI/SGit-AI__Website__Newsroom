# v0.13.31: @newsroom.writer: an analyst's blueprint for RiskMandate

**[An analyst's blueprint for RiskMandate](../../articles/an-analysts-blueprint-for-riskmandate.html)**: a power user's note from the WestCap CISO Summit, and our answer.

- **Where we agree.** Control what agents do, not just what they can access; the CISO question; starting small.
- **Who is already there.** Drata, Endor Labs, Socket, Bedrock Data, Sondera, AWS, the gateways, the testers and responders, from their own pages.
- **Their architecture, our pieces.** Discover, evaluate, enforce and prove, mapped onto the roadmap.
- **Not another box in the middle.** Hooks to block, rules compiled for the gateways customers run, and the policy they are configured from.
- **What we need now.** Customers, one workflow and one agent each.
