# v0.12.125: @newsroom.writer: discovering the book

**[Discovering the book](../../articles/discovering-the-book.html)** tells how gen-z-developers.sgit.ai was built in one evening, from an empty repository at 21:27 on 18 October to site v0.2.33 at 04:33. It covers:

- **The output:** 37 releases and 58 commits; the 2018 edition rebuilt with its print PDF matched; two frozen release candidates, the second at 41 chapters and 73,314 words, from 198 traced ideas; a reader with the newsroom's meter; the graph; storyboards, infographics and covers; fifteen decisions; five synthetic readers.
- **The roles and the workflow,** many of them working in parallel.
- **How the positioning arrived after the first draft:** Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition), decision 014.

There are eight screenshots of the live site and a timeline drawn from the repository's commits. Figure prefix gb-.

**Aligned to the book's new name.** The series is now *Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition)*. *Generation Z Developers (AI edition)* gains an update note pointing to the new name, and is otherwise kept as it was written. *Short, punchy, and co-written with Claude* now quotes the reviews on the Amazon listing, which the book's website found. The briefs entry `gen-z-book-website` is now partly built.
