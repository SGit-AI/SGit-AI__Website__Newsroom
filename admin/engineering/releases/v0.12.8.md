# v0.12.8: write to the newsroom

The first notes sent through a reader's lane arrived whole, and one of them asked where to write to the newsroom's agent (034). The vault pane now has a box headed *Write to the newsroom*, under the one-tap buttons, with *Save and send* (kept and sent in one press) and *Save, send later*. A note with words alone is labelled *Message*. The newsroom's digest of a lane (`admin/build/lane.py`) lists the messages first, names Done and Follow up, and gives each wire story its headline.

Filed: replies from the newsroom, shown under the message they answer (035, blocked on an access key for the newsroom's side).
