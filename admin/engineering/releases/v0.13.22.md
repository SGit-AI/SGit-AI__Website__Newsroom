# v0.13.22: @newsroom.writer: the Open Security Summit and its debt

**[The Open Security Summit and its debt](../../articles/the-open-security-summit-and-its-debt.html)**: how technical debt stalled a summit that treated every participant as a presenter, measured from its public repositories.

- **The model.** Working sessions and outcomes; in 2018, 180 people committed to the summit's website.
- **The code.** Each year copied the last; templates grew from about 5,200 to 9,600 lines and froze in 2022 while content kept coming.
- **The people.** From 180 committing in 2018 to 14 in 2024.
- **The follow-up.** 409 session pages and 194 recordings, and no personal path for each participant.
- **The restart.** Agents to map, personalise and follow up; London, online, and hosted projects; it needs funding.
- **Four charts.** Commits and people, code and content, sessions and videos, and the debt loop.
