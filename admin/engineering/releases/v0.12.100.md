# v0.12.100: @newsroom.ux: the reading strip reports whole milliseconds

`SgitReading.sections()` now reports whole milliseconds, as `save()` stores them. Before, a save landing while the page was paused rounded the fractional time not yet stored, so the total moved by under a millisecond with nothing counted, and the pause check in `test_reading.mjs` failed on one CI run.

The fix was first pushed under v0.12.99, whose tag was already taken, so that push was not deployed; this release carries it.
