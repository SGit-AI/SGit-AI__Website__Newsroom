# v0.13.2: @newsroom.writer: the RiskMandate roadmap

- **The roadmap.** Five stages of development, policies, understand, contain, manage and accelerate, then insurance.
- **Where we are.** The first stage, the policies and the flows, run with our first customers at customers.riskmandate.ai, whose own counts are quoted.
