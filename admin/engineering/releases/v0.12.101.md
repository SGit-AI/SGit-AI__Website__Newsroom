# v0.12.101: @newsroom.writer: allowed is not right, and the Northstar refund twin

An answer to Ashish Rajan's article on trustworthy AI systems, in three parts:

1. [Allowed is not right](../../articles/allowed-is-not-right.html): his Northstar refund, his four questions (SECURE, OBSERVE, RESPOND, VERIFY) mapped to the behaviour, decision and workflow policies, and what the twin showed.
2. [Inside the Northstar twin](../../articles/inside-the-northstar-twin.html): a tour of the vault, case by case.
3. [The Northstar refund twin](../../demos/vaults/northstar-twin/index.html): a new page, built by `admin/build/northstar_twin.py`, that embeds the vault (n89dyusm on dev.send.sgraph.ai) with its public read key, kept in `admin/content/vaults/northstar-twin.json`. The vault holds a deterministic engine, thirteen synthetic refund cases, four designs of ten controls, and an app to play, replay and compare every decision.

The new trail `northstar` joins them. The series on agent decision policies now gives each element five states, adding verified to observed, and the refund scenario in [three decisions, mapped](../../articles/three-decisions-mapped.html) gains the decision it lacked: the amount under the limit. Figures and screenshots use the new nt- prefix.
