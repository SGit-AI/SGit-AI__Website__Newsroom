# v0.12.128: @newsroom.writer: start a company, and can you sell it?

Two articles from Dinis's memos on the first strategic objective of a founder, written for the newsroom and for the book's company chapters. **[Start a company](../../articles/start-a-company.html)**: as soon as there is revenue, because companies and the contracts that govern them have to be learned by doing; the UK (£100 online since 1 February 2026, model articles by default), Portugal (organised accounts and a certified accountant required by law, even with no trading), and Lithuania (e-residency, still to be tried); the memo's "e-citizenship" corrected to e-residency, and its "quarterly statements" to the quarterly VAT return. **[Can you sell it?](../../articles/can-you-sell-it.html)**: sell what you have, however rough, because the point is to learn.
