# v0.12.69: @newsroom.writer: When journalism becomes intelligence, built, and its vault

One new article written here from a request, and a vault published on this site.

When journalism becomes intelligence, built takes Francesco Marconi's INMA deck and builds it: an observation desk for one data-center buildout, as an sgit vault app (hn1s3h6q, dev.send.sgraph.ai) that follows the eleven slides with an atlas of 12 families and 78 event types, a six-entity ontology, 16 synthetic source records, 17 observations and a community impact tracker, every illustration drawn as SVG in the deck's pen-and-ink style. The records are synthetic on purpose: a fictional twin of the deck's real example, so that no invented record carries a real agency's or company's name. Four figures.

New page: /demos/vaults/mesa-ranch-desk/, generated by admin/build/mesa_ranch_vault.py from admin/content/vaults/mesa-ranch-desk.json, which holds only the vault id and its public read key; it embeds the vault with assets/vault-ui-embed.js as sgit.ai's vault pages do, with a tour of screenshots. The vault key is not in the repository.
