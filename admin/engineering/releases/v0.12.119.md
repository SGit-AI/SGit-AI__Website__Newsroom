**[Short, punchy, and co-written with Claude](../../articles/short-punchy-and-co-written.html)** sets out what the second edition of Generation Z Developers has to be:

- a book for anyone getting into AI, with a practical chapter on the agentic email workflow;
- short and punchy, as readers liked the first edition;
- co-written with Claude and credited, with the ratio of memo to word shown (from *How much of this did I write?*: about 65,000 words in, 96,000 out);
