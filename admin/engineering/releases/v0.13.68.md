# v0.13.68: @newsroom.writer: process behaviour policies, and the sgit teams that come back

- **[Process behaviour policies](../../articles/process-behaviour-policies.html).** A new member of the behaviour policy family, built and measured in this newsroom's own cloud container: a policy derived from one traced run (files, system calls, programs, hosts), enforced by seven walls Linux already offers (an ephemeral user, an empty read-only root, namespaces, Landlock, seccomp, a network broker, cgroup limits). A probe that could do 13 of 14 things as root could do none under it, for 92 to 187 ms a run. Four figures, and the code, policies and results as [a data pack](../../articles/data/process-behaviour-policies__pack.json).
- **[A team frozen in time](../../articles/a-team-frozen-in-time.html), updated.** The sgit CLI and SG/Send teams as the main examples: sessions that resumed after 36 and 43 days without a commit, from the current code, straight into tested work, with a new timeline figure.
