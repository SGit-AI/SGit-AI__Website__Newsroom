# v0.12.67: @newsroom.writer: The expert founders

One new article written here from a voice memo.

The expert founders argues that generative AI changes who can start a company: the outsider playbook of the last twenty years (find a gap, raise on belief, hire the experts, displace the incumbents, become one) gives way to subject-matter experts who build the product themselves with agents. It backs the argument with founder-age and industry-experience data, the rise of solo founders, premature-scaling and venture-return data, and European and US funding comparisons, with their limits stated. Three examples: Dinis Cruz in security, Chinmay Gupte in knee health, and a founder in legal records whose link is to follow. Three figures.
