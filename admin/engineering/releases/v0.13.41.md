# v0.13.41: @newsroom.writer: Capture first, and the PHP story's own site on the front page

**[Capture first](../../articles/capture-first.html)**: the newsroom's first story covered while it was breaking. A BBC live page at breakfast became an evidence vault at 08:03 UTC, a section here at 08:13, and a site of its own at 09:27. By 12:05 the vault held 184 sources, 177 captures and 855 hashed files from six writers.

- **The argument.** Capture before you write: live pages, down pages and posts change within hours, so the vault came before the page, and captures are only ever added.
- **What went wrong.** Two collecting agents stopped before committing. Forty sources were recovered from their folders, and two rules follow.
- **Figures.** The morning in two lanes, from the BBC page, the captures and two git logs; and one vault with many writers, counted from every `source.json`.

**The PHP story's own site, [php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai](https://php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/), on the front page** (card 088): a Developing story strip in all three views, fed by `php-flaws/build/track.py`, which checks the live site. The same five checks are at the top of [/php-flaws/](../../php-flaws/index.html), which retires into a pointer when they pass. The [briefing pack](../../briefs/php-oct-2026.zip) for the site's team asks for `/status.json` and for the 40 recovered sources.

**One rule widened.** `admin/build/test_front.mjs` checks that the front page's own body links nowhere off the site. It now also allows `https://<story>.newsroom.sgit.ai/`, a story's own site under the newsroom's name, which this strip exists to link to.
