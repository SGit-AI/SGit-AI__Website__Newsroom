# v0.13.46: @newsroom.writer: the reader tests survive a slow apt mirror

**Why.** On 22 October the runners' Ubuntu mirror served the 26 MB of system packages Playwright's `install-deps` fetches at about 43 kB/s. The install step took 469 s, 612 s and 716 s instead of 24 to 41 s, and the reader-tests job hit its 12-minute limit mid-test in the runs for v0.13.44 and v0.13.45; v0.13.45 was never deployed.

- **The packages are cached.** The `.deb` files are kept between runs with `actions/cache`, handed to apt before `install-deps`, and saved after, so a slow mirror costs one run, not every run. apt retries a failed fetch three times.
- **The limit is 25 minutes.** Room for a cold cache on a slow mirror; a warm run should be back to about four minutes.
- **What this release also carries.** v0.13.45's update to [application behaviour policies](../../articles/application-behaviour-policies.html), with the PHP story site's compromise map.
