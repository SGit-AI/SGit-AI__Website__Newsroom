# v0.13.115: @newsroom.dev: independent review, a section

**A section to point to.** [newsroom.sgit.ai/reviews/](../../reviews/index.html) is now *Independent review*: how outside reviewers check this newsroom, in six steps (what to review, how, where the reviewer works, the gate, the desk, the record), then every review and every open finding. A bar across its pages links the queue, [Vaults and keys](../../reviews/vaults.html), the criteria, the send page and the records, and the section is in the menu under How it runs.

**Vaults and keys.** One page with every public part: each reviewer's vault and its read key, and the review desk's and the security agent's keys as pki.sgit.ai key links, each as a QR code; the key bundles and their register submissions; where the security agent's verdicts go; who does what; and the only private things, the keys that write or sign, named, with who holds each, none of them on the site.

**Secrets that fit a QR code.** Each secret is sealed to the editor's key on pki.sgit.ai, and is now small enough for one QR code: the desk and the security agent keep only the key they sign with. ChatGPT's vault (now gfzbzp41) and the two keys (sha256:fb35b723a2ae8b98, sha256:2f1a036313b78b1d) were made again for it; the first ones are listed as retired, with why.
