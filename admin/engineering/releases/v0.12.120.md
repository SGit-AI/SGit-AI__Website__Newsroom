# v0.12.120: @newsroom.writer: two years in, the opposite

He could neither buy the one essay nor answer it.

The article sets that against the newsroom's model, where trust is earned by reading and the reader pays for what they read, and against Dinis's own two years in AI: the most productive and empowered of his career, his inbox under control, hard and not linear, with Wardley maps showing the game.
