# v0.13.98

@newsroom.writer: review drafts. An article the editor has not approved yet can now be served without reaching the front page: `admin/build/drafts.py` writes `admin/content/drafts/<slug>.md` to `drafts/<slug>.html`, with the newsroom's chrome, `noindex, nofollow`, and no link from the home page, the article list or the feeds; the site's search skips `drafts/`. The first draft is waiting for the editor's review.
