# v0.13.24: @newsroom.writer: the attack surface you do not have

**[The attack surface you do not have](../../articles/the-attack-surface-you-do-not-have.html)**: why every CVE now makes Dinis smile.

- **Today's PHP flaws, and a month of critical server vulnerabilities.** From php.net, CISA's alerts and WordPress, none of them in this stack.
- **The attack surface compared.** Static files, the reader's browser, and one upload server that stores only ciphertext.
- **The constraint is speed.** 45 static sites in eleven weeks, a newsroom with payments, and more on the way.
- **Where the risk is going.** Internal applications, rarely tested like public ones.
- **The question to ask of every component.** Why does this need to be on the server?
- **Three figures.** The month of CVEs, the two attack surfaces, and what the constraint produced.
