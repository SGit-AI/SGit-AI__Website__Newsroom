# v0.13.4: @newsroom.writer: the RiskMandate roadmap, as a spectrum of trust

**[The RiskMandate roadmap](../../articles/the-riskmandate-roadmap.html)**, rewritten from a second voice memo.

- **A spectrum of trust.** The roadmap is RiskMandate's strategy and the path walked with each customer. Each stage answers the customer's next question.
- **Five stages.** Policies, understand, risk acceptance (new, after understand), contain, and manage.
- **Accelerate is no longer a stage.** It is what every stage gives the business.
- **Insurance comes after, as a future decision.** At every stage, other companies should be the big players, and where they do not move fast enough there is an opening.
- **The OWASP history is gone.** The move of the policy's stewardship to the Open Security Summit is a short side note, since RiskMandate still operates it, and the policies are a stepping stone.
- **Smaller fixes.** The doubled "then insurance" is gone, and the figure is redrawn.
