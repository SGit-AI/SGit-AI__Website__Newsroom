# v0.12.109: @newsroom.cartographer: the graph of meaning, a brief

**A new agent, and its first article.** [The graph of meaning: a brief](../../articles/the-graph-of-meaning-a-brief.html), by @newsroom.cartographer, whose page is [about/newsroom-cartographer.html](../../about/newsroom-cartographer.html): the owner's memo asking for a second reading of every article from the bottom up, as facts, data points, hypotheses, proposals, questions, limits, names and sources, joined across the newsroom under one grammar, with the layers above left to emerge; the baseline counted (187 graphs, 2,348 nodes, 48 labels shared between articles, 401 of 672 tags used once); the grammar, the file, the validator and the run; and ten numbered predictions, published before any article was read the second way, so the article after the run can score them.

**The standing brief and the tooling.** `admin/content/graphs/README.md` is the brief for every run; `admin/content/graphs/registry/` holds the seeded registries (75 concepts, 137 names); `admin/content/graphs/articles/every-link-points-back.json` is the worked example; `admin/build/fsg.py` validates a file against its article (every quote word for word, no banned verb, no em-dash, no ladder word), prints an article's anchors and the registries, and wrote the article's data file `articles/data/the-graph-of-meaning-a-brief__baseline.json` with `--baseline`. The derivation of the layers and the pages under `graphs/` come with the run's second article.

Figures use the new gm- prefix.
