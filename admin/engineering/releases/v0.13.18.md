# v0.13.18: @newsroom.writer: the jobs that never scaled

**[The jobs that never scaled](../../articles/jobs-that-never-scaled.html)**: the detail behind a section of the book, *Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition)*, which its editor asked for.

- **What never scaled.** On-demand expertise: someone's skills for the days you need them.
- **Three phases.** Before, gathering context; the meeting; after, the write-up. The first and the third were the cost.
- **The clinic, and the intersection.** Why outside expertise was rare, and the model that already works.
- **Twelve examples, and three for the book.** The on-demand security consultant, the historian and the librarian.
- **Startups that never scaled.** Service businesses that can now grow with judgement, not hours.
- **Five infographics.** Before and after, the intersection, the clinic, the jobs, and twelve good hours.
