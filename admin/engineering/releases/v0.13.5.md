# v0.13.5: @newsroom.writer: insure, the sixth stage of the roadmap

**[The RiskMandate roadmap](../../articles/the-riskmandate-roadmap.html)** now has six stages: policies, understand, risk acceptance, contain, manage, and insure.

- **Insure is a stage of its own.** Its question is "can our agents be covered?"
- **A new section: insurance is the end game.** Every stage produces evidence, insurance is where that evidence is worth the most, and so insurers are part of the play from the start, with the conversations beginning now.
- **The line about whether we would play in insurance is gone.**
- **Smaller changes.** The players section stands on its own, and the figure is redrawn with six stages.
