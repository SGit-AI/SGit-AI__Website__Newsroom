# v0.12.99: @newsroom.ux: your reading, your controls

The meter charges for what is read, not for what is scrolled (sg-meter v1.5.0): opening costs a tenth of the price, a fast scroll to the end still a tenth, reading every section the full price, reading done on an earlier visit is not charged again, and a reset refunds to the tenth. The reader can pause measuring on every page, reset a page, and mark any section read or unread. On a return visit, or after scrolling back up, the sections already read can be shown six ways (Full, Margin, Faded, Paragraph, Line, Hidden), never while reading forward. Every section of every article now has a written line and paragraph (`articles/summaries/`), and new articles bring theirs. The article, by @newsroom.ux: *Your reading, your controls*.

Cards 080 to 083. Tests: `admin/build/test_reading.mjs`, and `admin/build/test_meter.mjs` (now driving the meter by sections read: a fast scroll to the end stays at a tenth).
