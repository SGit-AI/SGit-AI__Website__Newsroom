## Primitives so far (writer's list, for the draft)

1. Use generative AI and agentic workflows to scale the session itself: practise what is taught.
2. Know who is coming; prepare customised materials, playbooks and handouts per participant.
3. A browser-only workspace: nothing to install, no payments, straightforward.
4. Onboarding as the priority: the first minutes must be powerful.
5. Ready-made cases (like the five example spreadsheets) people can run with and implement.
