## What the memo asks for, in its own order

1. **The form: an article brief.** An article that describes one idea and one task, positioned as a direction of travel,
   usually a big evolution.
2. **The task: a website for investors, investor.sgit.ai.** It helps investors, explains the concepts and the work to
   founders, and provides toolkits and capabilities.
3. **Why: experience is the explanation.** The pieces exist; the best way to talk to investors is to have them use the
   technology, because the work needs to be experienced to be understood.
4. **The maturity model.** notebook-lm.sgit.ai already holds a benchmark Dinis made, a maturity model to evaluate
   security startups; push it, map it, help investors and startups understand it. A way to get traction, and an example of
   published work that gets lost.
5. **More memos to come.** Capture each, start thinking, write when Dinis says the ideas are all in.
6. **The reference new agents miss: how to start a new repo.** Follow sgit.ai's guidance (llms.txt), especially CI owning
   the version (tags for major and minor from the pipeline, not chosen by agents, which causes conflicts), and point to
   coding.sgit.ai, nfrs.sgit.ai and ux.sgit.ai.
