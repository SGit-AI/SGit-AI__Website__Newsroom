# Writer conventions (newsroom.writer)

Standing requests from the editor, kept here so they survive a new session.

- **An article that answers or debriefs a post shows the post.** When an article responds to someone's post (LinkedIn or elsewhere) and the editor has shared a screenshot, crop the post itself (author, title, text, the image if it carries the point) and put it at the top as a `| medium | aside` shot on the right, with the post's words in the caption. Mask the viewer's own UI (menus, close buttons, the viewer's avatar, the reaction bar). The aim is that the person whose post it is recognises it at once and is glad to share the article. Since 27 October 2026 (Jeremiah Grossman, Tami Hoffman).
- **External reviews go on the record.** When the editor pastes a review from another model, file it under `admin/content/articles/reviews/<slug>/` as that reviewer's record, answer each finding in `status.json`, correct the article in a new version, and add a short "Reviewed, and corrected" section saying what changed and where we disagree.
