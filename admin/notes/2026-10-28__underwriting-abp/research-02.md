- Lloyd's coverholder: "A Lloyd's Coverholder, also referred to as a Managing General Agent (MGA), is a company that has been authorised" / "by a Lloyd's Managing Agent to enter into insurance contracts on behalf of a Lloyd's syndicate." "This authority is granted through a formal Binding Authority agreement". https://www.lloyds.com/market-resources/delegated-authorities/compliance-and-operations/about-coverholders (H)
- NAIC MGA model act #225 (M). NAIC reinsurance: "Reinsurance, often referred to as "insurance for insurance companies," is a contract between a reinsurer and an insurer." https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/reinsurance (H, 24 Oct 2025). Captive: "a captive is a wholly owned subsidiary created to provide insurance to its non-insurance parent company" https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/captive-insurance-companies (H, 5 Jan 2026).
- Lloyd's members: "A Lloyd's syndicate is formed by one or more members joining together to provide capital and accept insurance risks on a several liability basis." Capital "known as Funds at Lloyd's (FAL)". https://www.lloyds.com/market-resources/member-services/about/what-is-a-member (H)
- Fronting: "the licensed carriers whose balance sheets stand behind a growing share of MGA-placed business"; 2025 "more than $22 billion in gross premiums"; ceded "nearly $19 billion to nonaffiliated reinsurers". Insurance Business citing Conning, 31 Aug 2026, https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/breaking-news/conning-fronting-sectors-easy-growth-phase-is-ending-587956.aspx (H)
- Solvency II Art. 101(3): SCR "subject to a confidence level of 99,5 % over a one-year period." https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/rulebook/solvency-ii-single-rulebook/article-2188_en (H)
- PRA MCR: "must neither fall below 25% nor exceed 45% of the firm's SCR"; floors "£2,400,000" general, "£3,500,000" with classes 10 to 15. https://www.prarulebook.co.uk/pra-rules/minimum-capital-requirement/03-06-2026 (H). EU floors raised to €2.7m non-life (2021/C 423/12) (H, Dutch text).
- NAIC RBC: "a statutory minimum level of capital that is based on two factors"; "to identify potentially weakly capitalized companies." https://content.naic.org/cipr-topics/risk-based-capital (H)
- Coalition acquired a carrier 15 Aug 2022 ("licensed in all 50 states") https://www.coalitioninc.com/announcements/Coalition-completes-acquisition-of-insurance-carrier-with-licenses-in-all-50-states (H); Coalition Insurance Company launched 30 Jan 2023 (M).
- At-Bay, 24 Jan 2023: "we've taken a major step by acquiring At-Bay Specialty Insurance Company"; "Taking on balance sheet risk and owning our own insurance carrier are crucial steps on our journey." https://www.at-bay.com/articles/introducing-at-bay-specialty-insurance-company/ (H)
- Lemonade, 30 Jun 2026: "Under the renewed quota share agreements, Lemonade expects to cede approximately 18% of premium to reinsurers" https://www.lemonade.com/investor/news/lemonade-renews-reinsurance-program-improving-costs-coverage-and-capital-efficiency (H); about 55% in 2024-25 (M).
- EMD2 Art. 4: initial capital "of not less than EUR 350 000" https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/110/article/4 (H); EMR 2011 reg. 32: credit "provided that such credit is not granted from funds safeguarded in accordance with regulation 20" https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/99/regulation/32 (H).
