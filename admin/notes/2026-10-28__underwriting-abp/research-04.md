# Research 4: where to build the insurer, and what it costs (28 October 2026)

*Gathered by a research agent of newsroom.writer's session for article 4. H: fetched, quote word for word (some quotes
passed through a summarising fetch and were rechecked where used). M: snippet or secondary. U: not verified.*

## UK
- DLA Piper, last modified 22 Sep 2026 (H), https://intelligence.dlapiper.com/managing-general-agents/?c=GB: MGA routes "direct authorization by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) or operating as an Appointed Representative (AR)"; direct authorisation "can typically take up to nine months from when an applicant starts preparing its application"; capital "GBP5,000 or 2.5% of annual income from regulated insurance distribution activity" (the higher); "Professional indemnity insurance is required for FCA-authorized insurance intermediaries."
- Lloyd's coverholders need a sponsoring Lloyd's broker or managing agent (M).
- PRA New Insurer Start-up Unit, updated 12 Aug 2026 (H), https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/prudential-regulation/new-insurer-start-up-unit: "The PRA created a 'mobilisation' regime, effective from 31 December 2024, to facilitate new insurer expansion and entry into the UK market."; up to 12 months with restrictions.
- FCA Regulatory Sandbox (H): "the ability to test products and services in a controlled environment"; no AI or insurance cohort now.
- ISPV regime, PRA SS8/17, 2017, current 15 Nov 2024 (H).
## US
- NAIC Model 225 (H): MGA "Manages all or part of the insurance business of an insurer"; licensed "as a producer in this state"; bond "at least $100,000 or ten percent (10%) of the managing general agent's total annual written premium ... but in no event greater than $500,000."
- WSIA (H): surplus lines cover "unique risks for which admitted carriers do not offer a filed policy form or rate"; new products are "difficult, if not impossible, to price or rate for insurance purposes." https://www.wsia.org/wcm/wcm/About/What_is_Surplus_Lines.aspx
- AM Best via Insurance Business, 11 Sep 2026 (H): "The surplus lines market closed 2025 at a record $143.3 billion in direct premium written".
- Conning via Reinsurance News, 29 Jul 2026 (H): US MGA market "reached approximately $128 billion in 2025"; "approximately 20% of total US MGA premium is supported through fronting carrier relationships." https://www.reinsurancene.ws/conning-estimates-us-mga-premiums-reached-128bn-in-2025/
- Vermont 8 V.S.A. § 6004(a) (H): pure captive "not less than $250,000.00"; sponsored captive "not less than $100,000.00".
## EU
- Central Bank of Ireland (H): "the principle of a single registration"; operate "throughout the European Union".
- EIOPA Note on Competitiveness, 17 Jun 2026 (H): "insurers are increasingly outsourcing key functions to MGAs, creating concerns about weak oversight, conflicts of interests, unclear responsibility for product governance and difficulties in cross-border supervision."; EIOPA "could engage with the Lloyd's market to better understand how we can build capacity in the EU".
- Malta protected cells (M only).
## Bermuda
- BMA licensing (H, undated), https://bma.bm/insurance-licensing: Class 3A "Small commercial insurers", capital with "a $1 million floor"; Class 4 "$100 million floor"; IIGB "intends to carry on general business in an innovative manner"; sandbox IGB "a company aiming to carry on general business in an innovative and experimental manner".
- BMA sandbox (H): Breach Insurance became IIGB 3 Feb 2023; Ensuro Re IIGB 30 Apr 2024.
- Bermuda Stock Exchange, Insurance Business 11 Feb 2026 (H): "more than 93% of global issuance"; "new cyber catastrophe bonds".
- 15% corporate income tax from 2025, groups of €750m revenue or more only (M).
## Middle East
- DIFC/DFSA: site 403; intermediaries Category 4, base capital US$10,000 (M).
- ADGM FSRA proposal, Reinsurance News 18 Dec 2025 (H): insurers to hold "120% of the ASCR under normal circumstances". Captive base capital in the 2015 draft rules: US$150,000 to US$1 million (H text, M currency).
- Saudi Arabia: Insurance Authority from 2023 (M); law as amended 2021: insurer minimum paid-up capital "shall not be less than three hundred million Saudi riyals" (H).
