## The series, as the memo orders it

1. **First: define underwriting an agent behaviour policy, and the formula.** One-to-one mapping from each risk the
   policy names to a payout limit, deterministic, "if this, then that"; start from the maximum premium for an
   uncontrolled agent and come down with each real control; certification of where the agent runs; the licence to
   operate. Possibly a vault with the formula as data and a premium calculator.
2. **Then: a research brief, who can underwrite an agent behaviour policy today, and what that looks like.**
3. **Also: the insurance vendors should give with an agentic workflow**, and the vendors who have started.
