# Research 3: the vendors' share (28 October 2026)

*Gathered by a research agent of newsroom.writer's session for article 3. H: fetched, quote word for word. M: snippet or
secondary. U: not verified. EUR-Lex was read through the EU Publications Office (Cellar); OpenAI's site returned 403.*

- EU Product Liability Directive (EU) 2024/2853 (OJ 18.11.2024), https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/2853/oj (H):
  Art. 4(1) "'product' means all movables, even if integrated into, or inter-connected with, another movable or an immovable; it includes electricity, digital manufacturing files, raw materials and software";
  Recital 13 "A developer or producer of software, including AI system providers within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 ... should be treated as a manufacturer.";
  Art. 11(2) the later-defect defence does not apply to "(b) software, including software updates or upgrades" or "(c) a lack of software updates or upgrades necessary to maintain safety", "provided that it is within the manufacturer's control";
  Recital 32 "A product can also be found to be defective on account of its cybersecurity vulnerability";
  Art. 15 "Member States shall ensure that the liability of an economic operator pursuant to this Directive is not, in relation to the injured person, limited or excluded by a contractual provision or by national law.";
  Art. 22(1) transpose "by 9 December 2026"; Art. 2(1) applies "to products placed on the market or put into service after 9 December 2026"; Art. 5: claimants are "any natural person".
- AWS shared responsibility model, https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/shared-responsibility-model/ (H, undated): "Security and Compliance is a shared responsibility between AWS and the customer."; "this differentiation of responsibility is commonly referred to as Security 'of' the Cloud versus Security 'in' the Cloud."
- Anthropic Commercial Terms, effective 17 Jun 2025, https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms (H): liability "is limited to Fees paid by Customer for the Services in the previous 12 months."; D.3 "It is Customer's responsibility to evaluate whether Outputs are appropriate for Customer's use case, including where human review is appropriate"; indemnity K.1 for third-party IP claims.
- Salesforce MSA, last updated 15 Oct 2026, https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/documents/legal/salesforce_MSA.pdf (H): 10.1 aggregate liability not to exceed "THE TOTAL AMOUNT PAID BY CUSTOMER AND ITS AFFILIATES HEREUNDER FOR THE SERVICES GIVING RISE TO THE LIABILITY IN THE TWELVE MONTHS PRECEDING THE FIRST INCIDENT OUT OF WHICH THE LIABILITY AROSE."; indemnity 9.1 IP only.
- Salesforce AI Acceptable Use Policy, 11 Dec 2025 (H): 6.A "Customer is responsible for determining if its use of these technologies is safe."
- No Agentforce warranty or insurance for agent actions found (U for absence).
- OpenAI Copyright Shield, DevDay 6 Nov 2023, copyright only, enterprise and API (M; OpenAI's page not fetched).
- Zendesk, 28 Aug 2024 (H): "customers will only incur costs for issues that are resolved autonomously by AI." https://www.zendesk.com/newsroom/articles/zendesk-outcome-based-pricing/
- AIUC-1: Cursor certified 12 Aug 2026, KPMG aIQ Capture 27 Aug 2026, Sierra 17 Sep 2026 (H, aiuc-1.com); only ElevenLabs documented with AIUC-1-backed insurance. AIUC's own site: "We've raised $55M" (H, aiuc.com); FinTech Global reported $40M (H): conflict, use the company's figure and say so.
- EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (H): Art. 3(3) provider places a system on the market "under its own name or trademark"; Art. 3(4) deployer "using an AI system under its authority"; Art. 26(1) deployers use systems "in accordance with the instructions for use"; Art. 26(2) "assign human oversight to natural persons who have the necessary competence, training and authority"; Art. 25 a deployer becomes a provider after "a substantial modification".
