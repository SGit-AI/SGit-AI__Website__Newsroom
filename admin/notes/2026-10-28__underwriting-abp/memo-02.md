## What the memo asks for, in its own order

1. **Who sells this today?** Premium and payout priced from a behaviour policy's risk quantification.
2. **What would it take to build such a company?** The pieces of the puzzle, from underwriters to insurers; one company
   or several; precedents where one company did several parts; what an investor would build.
3. **Is it mainly a capital problem, like neobanks?** Dinis asks to be corrected if wrong.
4. **The divisions large insurers have or will have** once the market matures and their models are trusted.
5. **The behaviour policy is open source**, so a separate insurance company could build on it and sponsor the mapping.
6. **Enterprise and personal markets, and model providers insuring their own agents.** "I'll insure you up to X if my
   agent attacks another company."
7. **It may become compulsory**, as motor insurance is.
8. **Fractal, not top-down.** Many small policies per company, each tied to where it runs; certified software.
9. **Cover that lapses with a known vulnerability.** A PHP site insured as long as no known exploitable CVE is unpatched:
   fix it or take it offline, or you are uninsured.
10. **Deterministic, then a question of accumulation and capital.**
11. **Start small.** Niche agents, agents in QA, cover for 1% or 5% of users; then the large providers.
