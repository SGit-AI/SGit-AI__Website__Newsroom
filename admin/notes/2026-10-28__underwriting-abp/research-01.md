# Research 1: who insures agents, how other lines price, and what vendors cover (28 October 2026)

*Gathered by a research agent of newsroom.writer's session on 28 October 2026 for the underwriting series. H: the page
was fetched and the quote is word for word. M: from a search snippet or a secondary source; paraphrase only. U: not
verified; do not print as fact.*

## Insurers of AI agents and models (for article 2)

- AIUC: seed of $15M led by Nat Friedman; policies "cover customers and vendors in the event an agent causes harm"; pricing "reflects how safe the system is"; Kvist: "Just like consumers get a better car insurance rate for having airbags and anti-lock brakes." Fortune, 23 Jul 2025, https://fortune.com/2025/07/23/ai-agent-insurance-startup-aiuc-stealth-15-million-seed-nat-friedman/ (H)
- ElevenLabs, first AIUC-1-backed policy for voice agents: "these AI voice agents - and their actions - can now be insured, just like any other employee"; 5,835 tests across 14 risk categories; no insurer or limits named. 12 Feb 2026, https://elevenlabs.io/blog/aiuc-announcement (H)
- AIUC Series A, $40M led by Ribbit; AIUC-1 certified include Cursor, ElevenLabs, Harvey, KPMG, Lovable, UiPath, Fin. FinTech Global, 17 Sep 2026, https://fintech.global/2026/09/17/aiuc-raises-40m-to-police-risk-in-frontier-ai-agents/ (H)
- AIUC "limits up to $50M", Beazley capacity, premium tied to audit score: aggregators only (U).
- Armilla, Lloyd's coverholder, affirmative AI liability "underwritten by certain underwriters at Lloyd's, including Chaucer". 30 Apr 2025, https://www.armilla.ai/resources/armilla-launches-affirmative-ai-liability-insurance-with-lloyds-underwriter-chaucer (H). Limits "up to $25 million per organization", "AI Agent Failures: Claims arising from incorrect decisions, improper tool use, or escalation errors"; release dated 21 Jan 2026 in its header and "January 21, 2025" in its body: check. https://www.armilla.ai/resources/armilla-ai-raises-lloyds-backed-coverage-to-25m-as-traditional-insurers-retreat-from-ai-risk (H)
- Munich Re aiSure, since 2018; "Based on historical performance data, we can estimate the future likelihood of model underperformance."; "The quality of the model and its performance stability determine the premium."; "developed methods that enable us to automatically price the model performance risk of AI in real time"; "performance warranties enable you to indemnify your clients for their financial losses". https://www.munichre.com/en/solutions/for-industry-clients/insure-ai.html and /faq.html (H, undated)
- Testudo, Lloyd's coverholder; third-party claims from "AI-generated outputs"; "$9.25m per insured" with Apollo, Atrium, QBE, via Gallagher Re. FinTech Global, 9 Mar 2026, https://fintech.global/2026/03/09/testudo-expands-ai-liability-capacity-to-9-25m/ (H)
- No insurer found pricing agent cover from live agent controls or telemetry; the "Underwriting the Agent Economy" paper proposes telematics-like monitoring (M).

- Salesforce Agentforce: nothing found (U).

## Other lines (used in article 1)

- ingenie: "We'll collect data on your speed, acceleration, braking and cornering."; data helps "work out your renewal price". https://www.ingenie.com/how-it-works (H, undated)
- Howden, black box myths: night driving restricted "usually between 10pm and 6am"; poor driving may "increase your premium". 1 Sep 2025, https://www.howdeninsurance.co.uk/personal/motor/young-driver/guides/black-box-insurance-myths/ (H)
- Savings figures for black boxes (BIBA, ABI): secondary only (U).
- UK private medical insurance: moratorium (no cover for conditions in the five years before joining until two years trouble-free) or full medical underwriting (history disclosed, exclusions set before the policy starts). AXA Health and Bupa pages, snippets only (M).
- Howden single-article limit "usually between £1,000 to £2,500"; "declare any expensive items to your insurer". 9 Jan 2025, https://www.howdeninsurance.co.uk/personal/home/home-insurance/guides/single-article-limit/ (H)
- MoneySuperMarket: "You may need BS3621 standard five-lever mortice deadlocks on all your external doors in order to get home insurance."; "you are unlikely to be able to claim on your home insurance if you leave your doors unlocked." Updated 3 Sep 2026, https://moneysupermarket.com/home-insurance/house-locks (H)
