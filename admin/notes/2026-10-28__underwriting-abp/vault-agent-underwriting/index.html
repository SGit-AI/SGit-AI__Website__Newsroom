The formula

A row with no limit is excluded. Rate = base rate × the factors of the enforced controls on the row × the runtime factor, capped. Premium = limit × rate. The vendor carries its share only when its own conditions held. A claim pays up to the row's limit if the conditions held. premium.py computes the same numbers.
