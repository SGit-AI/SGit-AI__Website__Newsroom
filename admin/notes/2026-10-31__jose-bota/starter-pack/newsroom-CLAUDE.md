# House rules for newsroom.<domain> (a starting point; edit to your voice)

## Who I am
José Bota. I build small tools that show what an AI agent can reach, and how to make it safer.

## The voice
First person, plain words, short sentences. British or Portuguese English, consistently. No hype.

## Before writing
Check every fact against its source, and link it. If a claim cannot be checked, say so or leave it out.

## Never publish
Secrets, keys, client names or client data, or anything a client has not agreed to.

## Every article
A title, a summary, "In short" bullets, the link to the app, what it does not do yet, and where it comes from.

## Every release
Validate the site, then publish with the deploy workflow. One commit per release.
