## Questions to put on the record

- **WIRED:** the byline; the rule behind 72 hours; which NIST listing flagged the site, and whether it is a site or a product; how a password gave administrative access past the normal login on a network that uses smart cards; where the agent ran; which vulnerability was patched; whether the ban on Claude covers contractors.
- **Accenture:** who conducted the independent investigation; what was found without merit; when the incident was reported to the DOD.
- **DOD:** whether the incident was reported within 72 hours; whether the ban on Claude applies to contractors.
- **Anthropic:** whether the use described was through a consumer account, and what Anthropic could see or do about it.
- **AI Weekly:** the attribution of "We take all concerns seriously".
