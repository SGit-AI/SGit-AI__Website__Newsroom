Engineering: issues and releases

What is asked, what is broken, what shipped, and in which release: one card per issue, as files in the repository, rendered here.

The work on this site, tracked as files in the repository (admin/engineering/), the way Issues-FS-lite does it: one markdown card per issue with front matter, three folders (open, blocked, done) whose path is the status, and one file per release naming the cards it carries. Each card says what is asked or what happens, the scenario in Gherkin, a mock-up before it is built, and the outcome and screenshots after. Every change to the site is connected to a card, and the release history names the ids. Where a card came from is in its source: the owner’s feedback through the vault pane’s lane, a screenshot, a note in chat, a brief on sgit.ai, a test.
