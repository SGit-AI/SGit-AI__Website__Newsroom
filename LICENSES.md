or content.

> unless a specific document states otherwise.

## What that means here

| Path | Licence |

## The decision behind it

Per a decision of 21 August 2026, applied across the `*.sgit.ai` estate: unless a document
explicitly says otherwise, content on a `*.sgit.ai` website is released under CC BY 4.0.

## One limit specific to this site

**Facts this site reports about a sibling site are not this site's to license.** A version
number, a hostname, a release note — each is cited to the sibling's own repository, per the
Librarian role's sourcing rule (see `/roles/index.html`), and is not reproduced wholesale.
Only this site's own writing about those facts — the stories, the analysis, the boundary
statements — is CC BY 4.0 content of this repository.
