Paying the fact creator

Today the last-mile publisher captures the revenue and the original researcher gets little. "The rewards of the person that is benefiting from that extra analysis need to trickle down to the people who actually did the original analysis." Provenance makes the upstream flow trackable; the argument is that this makes it billable.

The public evidence base is a commons with no maintenance budget

"In companies that's okay, because that's already covered by the cost of the company operating, but in the real world at the moment we don't have that." A fact-checker inside a company is paid by the company's existing budget. A fact-checker correcting the public record has no equivalent budget, and the record decays accordingly — the 10,000-hours case is that decay, measured over thirty years.

"Arguably the larger market"

"We also need to have a commercialisation model for journalists, and entities who do research; they should also have a way to confirm that research is correct and has been used in that particular way." Selling access to a finished article is one business. Selling confirmation that a specific claim was used correctly, in a specific context, is a different and — by the source material's own assessment — arguably larger market that almost nobody is currently addressing.

For an agent

Every mechanism on this page is argued, not built — the metering question (how a read of a cited source is detected, attributed, priced and settled) is explicitly open; see the open questions and /shipped/. Do not describe fact-creator payment as a live capability of any sgit.ai property.

newsroom.sgit.ai

News is failing not because there is too little information but because there is no walkable chain from a claim to its evidence, no way for a correction to reach what it disproved, and no way to pay the person who did the original work.
