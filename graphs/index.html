The dimensions

A layer is a new dimension when it needs its own types and verbs. The same vocabulary seen from further away is the same dimension at less resolution, and the build says so rather than inventing a level.

  1. 0The article

    As the owner wrote it: the text, its sections and figures. Not derived here; it is the input.

    types: section, paragraph, figure
    verbs: contains, follows, illustrates
  2. 1The claims open

    What one article states, each claim anchored to its section and a quote. A new dimension: the words are not the article's.

    types: fact, data-point, hypothesis, proposal, question, definition, decision, limit
    verbs: backs, gives_rise_to, answers, limits, contradicts, supersedes, specifies, and their inverses
  3. 2The names and the vocabulary open

    The things in the world and the newsroom's own ideas that claims are about, one id each across every article. A new dimension: a thing, not a sentence about it. Names and concepts share the verbs, so they are one dimension, not two.

    types: 13 entity types; concept
    verbs: names / named_in, uses / used_in, defines / defined_in
  4. 3The sources open

    What the articles rest on outside the newsroom, dated as the article dates it. Its own types and one verb of its own: a dimension, a thin one.

    types: post, paper, documentation, article, repository, vault, talk, standard, law, news, book, page, video, dataset
    verbs: cites / cited_by
  5. 4Time open

    The dates the articles turn on. One type, one verb: a dimension by the rule, and the thinnest.

    types: date
    verbs: happened_on / dates
  6. 3·Gravity, clusters, the cut open

    Queries over dimension 2: which ids two or more articles share, how they cluster when the articles they share are counted, where one cut falls. The vocabulary did not change, so this is dimension 2 at less resolution, not a new one.

    types: none new: the nodes are the names and concepts of dimension 2
    verbs: shares_articles_with (derived, weighted)

    not a new dimension: same types and verbs as the layer below, less resolution

  7. 5The peaks open

    The natural places of arrival: a name or idea with high gravity that also bridges clusters. Needed its own words, so the build calls it a dimension, and lists them.

    types: peak
    verbs: arrives_at / overlooks
  8. EThe editor's layers open

    Drawn by hand before the run (six topics, five desks and 44 subjects, eleven series, the sections, the personas). The control the clusters and peaks are scored against.

    types: topic, desk, subject, series, section, persona
    verbs: placed_in / holds

The pages

  • The map: the names and ideas with gravity, laid out and cut in two, and turned in three dimensions.
  • The peaks: where readers arrive, by gravity and bridging.
  • The clusters, against the editor's layers: what emerged, scored against the desks, topics, sections and personas.
  • The claims: every hypothesis and question in the newsroom, and the shape of the facts.
  • The names and the vocabulary: who and what the articles are about, with their gravity.
  • The sources and the dates.
  • The births: when each idea first appeared, and how many each article added.
  • Every article as facts and hypotheses: one page per article.
  • The standing brief: the grammar, the schema, the rules, the run.
  • The emergence report: every number of this run, as the second article scored them.

The data

Every page here is a projection of these files, which an agent can read instead: report.json (the numbers), map.json (the nodes with gravity, their layout, cluster and side), claims.json, names.json, sources.json, time.json, edges.json (the whole join as typed edges with inverses). The source files are one per article in the repository, admin/content/graphs/articles/<slug>.json, with the registries beside them.

Run 2026-10-18, built 2026-10-18 14:31 UTC, in 9.0 seconds. The articles that set and scored the expectations: The graph of meaning: a brief and The graph of meaning, drawn.

For an agent

Start with /graphs/data/report.json for the numbers and /graphs/data/edges.json for the whole join; the schema of a source file is in /graphs/brief.html.
