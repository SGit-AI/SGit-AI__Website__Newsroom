The map: the newsroom's names and ideas, cut in two

One picture of the whole: every name and idea two or more articles share, placed by the articles they share, and cut in two by the graph itself rather than by a hand.

The names and ideas in two or more articles, joined by the articles they share. In the picture below, left to right is the cut's own axis: every node's place along the Fiedler vector of the graph, which is what divides it in two, with the bridges in the middle; up and down is a force layout. The colours are the cut made without the frame, the names and ideas in more than a quarter of all articles, which are drawn grey. The interactive map under it is the same data, drawn in the page from map.json with no library: the cut axis, a force layout or the spectral one, coloured by side or by cluster, hover for a name and click for its page; and a turn in three dimensions on a canvas.

The SGit Newsroom: one page, one argument, with the figures, the data and the links to check it.
