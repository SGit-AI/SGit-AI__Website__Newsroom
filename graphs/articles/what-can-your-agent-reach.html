Definitions (3)

  • definitionRiskMandate writes down, for one agent in one deployment, the grant, the mandate, the delta and the barrier for every capability; that document is the Agent Behaviour Policy, and it carries no score and no verdict.“It carries no score and no verdict. It is the record.” § what
    Behaviour policy 91Reach 47RiskMandate 97abp.sgit.ai 23
  • definitionThere are four kinds of barrier, none, expectation, setting and boundary, and only a boundary, something enforced above the grant and out of the agent's reach, bounds anything.“There are four kinds, and only one of them bounds anything” § what
    Behaviour policy 91Hope or enforcement 65
  • definitionA decision policy says, at a branch point, who decides, on what evidence, within what limits and who is called when out of bounds; a workflow policy says which steps run in what order.“A workflow policy says which steps run in what order.” § decisions
