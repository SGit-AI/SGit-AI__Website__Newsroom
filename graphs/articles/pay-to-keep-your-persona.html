Hypotheses (4)

  • hypothesisAll three reasons work to a degree, but they do not scale for anyone who is not already enormous, and they make the reader feel the wrong thing.“they do not scale for anyone who is not already enormous, and that they make the reader feel the wrong thing” § top
    Micropayments and the reading meter 41Journalism and the news business 21
  • hypothesisWhat people will pay for is a persona that follows them to their phone, their laptop and their agent, so that connecting through Claude reads as their security persona and not as a stranger.“They pay because they want to keep their persona, and to have it wherever they read.” § top

  • hypothesisH7: readers who have more than one persona pay more often than readers who have one.“Readers who have more than one persona pay more often than readers who have one” § top

Definitions (2)

  • definitionWhat a reader is buying is not the article, which is free, but time, context, focus, the ability to make good decisions, a streamlined way through and a better experience.“The article is free to read here, and it is free to read with the meter below zero.” § top
