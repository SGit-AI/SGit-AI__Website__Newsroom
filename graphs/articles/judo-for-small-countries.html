Hypotheses (7)

  • hypothesisThe race for the biggest model, the most GPUs and the largest data centre is one a small country cannot win and should not enter; on a Wardley map that layer is already a commodity and only capital plays there.“Those components sit at the far right, in commodity, with a lead held by companies that have invested amounts a small country's whole technology budget would not match.” § race
    Wardley maps 24Strategy and positioning 26Portugal 10Romania 2Estonia
  • hypothesisWhat stops a talented person in a small country selling to the world is not talent, energy or price but the plumbing: forming a company, hiring across a border, being paid across Europe, working in another language, a contract, a tax form.“None of that is the service. All of it is logistics, legal and operational plumbing” § plumbing
    Plumbing as infrastructureEurope 6
  • hypothesisIn the age of agents, the plumbing for providing services to the outside world is infrastructure in the road sense, and a state that builds it enables every one of its people at once.“the plumbing for providing services to the outside world is infrastructure in exactly the road sense” § infrastructure
    Plumbing as infrastructureWardley maps 24
  • hypothesisYou cannot have sovereignty without open source and open schemas, because sovereignty means you can leave: run a parallel service, move the data and keep going.“You cannot have sovereignty without open source and open schemas, because sovereignty means you can leave.” § open
    Privacy and sovereignty 45Open source and open licences 53
  • hypothesisIf a hyperscaler, a payment provider or a model API went away tomorrow most European organisations would have to stop, because no market for the alternative exists and markets do not appear overnight.“because no market for the alternative exists, and markets do not appear overnight” § demand
    Incidents and resilience 25Europe 6
  • hypothesisMost value is captured by a model good enough for one job, in one language, with one culture in it; there is a genesis market for models for each Portuguese, Romanian and Estonian, with its culture in the training data and not just its spelling.“There is a market, still at genesis on the map, for models for Portuguese, and not one Portuguese” § models
    Smaller models, more of themPortuguese as a programming language 3Portugal 10Romania 2Estonia
  • hypothesisA small country that builds the plumbing makes the jobs available everywhere inside it, where most people want to live, and that is a competitive advantage its big neighbours cannot copy quickly.“A small country that builds the plumbing makes the jobs available everywhere inside it” § workforce
    Plumbing as infrastructureStrategy and positioning 26

Proposals (5)

  • proposalWhat the state pays for should belong to the commons: not that everyone changes the production system, but that the thing the public paid for is available to the public, which is how a local industry grows around infrastructure it can see.“It follows that what the state pays for should belong to the commons.” § open
    Open source and open licences 53Plumbing as infrastructureThe European Union 19
  • proposalCreate the demand before the day you need it: run parallel services, buy the local or open alternative for part of the estate now and accept fewer features, using procurement, the lever a state already holds.“Procurement is the lever a state already holds; it does not need a new law to prefer open schemas, data mobility and service mobility in what it buys.” § demand
    Incidents and resilience 25Open source and open licences 53Strategy and positioning 26
  • proposalThe playbook is five moves: map the attrition, commoditise the plumbing, create the demand, put the money where the market is still genesis, regulate what settled, then map again.“map the attrition, commoditise the plumbing, create the demand, put the money where the market is still genesis, regulate what settled, then map again” § playbook
    Wardley maps 24Strategy and positioning 26Plumbing as infrastructure
  • proposalRegulate what settled: once a layer is a commodity write the rule for it, data mobility, open interfaces, the report a failure must produce; the rule comes after the market, not before it.“The rule comes after the market, not before it.” § playbook
    Regulation and law 32Wardley maps 24
  • proposalAnyone in an embassy, ministry, company or university of these countries who wants to map the attrition for their own case can write to agent@riskmandate.ai.“and want to map the attrition for your own case” § limits
    RiskMandate 97Wardley maps 24
