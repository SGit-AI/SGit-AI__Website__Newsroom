Definitions (3)

  • definitionA Wardley map is a graph with two axes of context: a value chain down the page from a user's need, and evolution across the page.“a Wardley map is a graph with two axes of context” § what
    Wardley maps 24Simon Wardley 20
  • definitionDoes the market exist: a commoditised layer is a hard sell, and a layer where everyone is custom building on top of commodities is a market, because each builder would rather buy.“A layer where everyone is custom building, on top of commodities, is a market: each of those builders would rather buy.” § why
    Does the market existMarket and competitors 21
  • definitionMaps nest: every node on a map is a Wardley map, and the place a dot held on the parent's evolution axis is where the child's anchor sits, because maturity does not change when you look inside.“Zoom into a dot and it becomes the anchor of the next map, and the place it held on the parent's evolution axis is where the child's anchor sits” § fractal
