Proposals (5)

  • proposalThe engagement path is a free conversation, a first run for pounds, two hours or a day of expert time, a four-day pilot in the client's environment, then the maintained setup.“Each step is small enough to say yes to, and each ends with something they keep.” § path
    Pricing and economics 61AgenticsStartups and early access 34
  • proposalAlongside the offers, a marketplace: a small curated list of consultants who deliver agentic engagements on this stack, where a client picks the project type first and then the people.“a small, curated list of consultants who deliver agentic engagements on this stack” § marketplace
    A marketplace of consultantsAgentics
  • proposalConsultants join by six steps copied from markets that work: apply, show proof of work, peer review, interview, a shadowed pilot, and standards including a behaviour policy for consultants.“The markets that work have converged on the same few steps, and we can copy them.” § marketplace
    A marketplace of consultantsBehaviour policy 91
