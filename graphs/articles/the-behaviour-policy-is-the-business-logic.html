Facts (16)

  • factThe first rules anybody writes for an agent are mechanical and the same in every company: do not send email, only draft it; do not delete anything; use your own account.“They are short, they are the same in every company, and they are the rules I wrote first” § top

  • factA few questions in, which drafts may be sent, to whom, whether a price may be mentioned, you are no longer describing an agent but the business.“A few questions in, you are no longer describing an agent. You are describing the business.” § top
    Behaviour policy 91Policies inside policies
  • factBelow the first layer or two of an Agent Behaviour Policy what is captured is business logic, much of it never written down, which the agent-control tools stop before and which decides whether an agent can be trusted with real work.“The tools that sell agent controls mostly stop before it.” § top

Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisThe behaviour policy is a good place for vendors to integrate rather than a threat: a vendor enforces the parts of the written policy its product can reach, and several vendors can each own a row.“it gives their controls a rule to enforce and a number to move” § top

Proposals (2)

  • proposalThe structure that holds this is fractal: behaviour policies inside behaviour policies, each layer refining the one below inside the room it allows, from foundational controls up to what the company is for.“behaviour policies inside behaviour policies, each layer refining the one below, inside the room the one below allows” § top

Definitions (3)

  • definitionAbove the mechanics are four business layers: the function, how this firm does email; the process, the steps work goes through; the relationship, who this customer is to us now; and the organisation, what the company is for.“Go one layer up and the rules stop being the same in every company.” § top
    Policies inside policiesBehaviour policy 91
  • definitionFor every rule the policy records which of four things stands in the way: nothing, an expectation in prose enforced by nobody, a setting the agent's own account can flip, or a boundary; only the boundary is a control.“Only the last is a control.” § top
    Hope or enforcement 65Behaviour policy 91
  • definitionAdding whether a named person has accepted the risk with an expiry puts every rule in one of three states: backed by a control, governed by an accepted risk, or resting on hope.“every rule lands in one of three states: backed by a control, governed by an accepted risk, or resting on hope” § top
