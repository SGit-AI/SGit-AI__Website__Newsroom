Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisAgents change what source control is for: the clients are always online, the volume is machine-scale, and the expensive thing is moving bytes nobody needs.“Agents change what source control is for: the clients are always online, the volume is machine-scale, and the expensive thing is moving bytes nobody needs.” § means
    Multi-agent designs 62Vault design 48

Proposals (1)

  • proposalThe useful next steps are on the client: a small encrypted record of the commit chain, blame as a fold over path history, and testing at a much larger scale.“a small encrypted record of the commit chain so a partial clone does not walk history, blame as a fold over path history, and testing at a much larger scale.” § means
