Definitions (2)

  • definitionThe passkey is a lock, not an identity: the key pairs are generated at random and stored, never derived from the passkey, so everything that represents the person survives any change of lock, path, provider fix or recovery.“The passkey is a lock, not an identity. It opens a door and does nothing else.” § design
    The passkey is a lock, not an identityIdentity and secrets 59Personas and personalisation 33
  • definitionAccounts with no server and no identity: a static site gets a user who can open their own data on any device and keep it private from the operator, with no account database, password, email or back end.“No account database, no password, no email, no back end.” § enables
    Accounts with no serverClient-side and on-device 56Privacy and sovereignty 45
