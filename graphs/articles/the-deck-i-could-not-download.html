Facts (12)

  • factThe same agreement grants a royalty-free, sublicensable licence to monetise, charge for, restrict access to and train machine learning and large language models on the work.“train machine learning, large language, or other statistical or data models” § top
    ScribdRegulation and law 32
  • factThe general terms forbid users from training large language models on the same content: the right the platform reserves for itself it withholds from its users.“The right the platform reserves for itself it withholds from its users.” § top
    ScribdRegulation and law 32
  • factWhen the paywall arrived in September 2021 authors found an account-level checkbox allowing download without a subscription, which one writer reported as off by default.“which one writer who documented it in October 2021 reported as off by default” § top
    ScribdSlideShare
  • factFifteen years of embeds, backlinks and search ranking are inertia in Wardley's sense, and a stronger moat than the technology.“Simon Wardley's observation is the right one: "Past success breeds inertia."” § top
    Simon Wardley 20InertiaWardley maps 24
  • factThe complaint is easy to overstate: the uploader keeps copyright, the licence is non-exclusive, and the owner uploaded most decks under a Creative Commons licence.“The uploader keeps copyright. The licence is non-exclusive.” § top
    Creative Commons 14Open source and open licences 53
  • factThe three complaints, a subscription sold for one file, no say and no share, and not knowing where the work went, are all allowed by the licence; the service an author would choose does not exist.“The licence allows all three, which is why the complaint is not that anyone broke a rule.” § top
    SlideShareProvenance and evidence 99
  • factThe European Data Protection Board's guidelines say the right of access includes inferred and derived data, including personal data created by a service provider.“includes both inferred and derived data, including personal data created by a service provider” § top
    European Data Protection Board 3GDPR 8
  • factThe Court of Justice ruled in January 2023 that a controller must name the actual recipients of personal data where it can, not only categories.“The Court of Justice ruled in January 2023 that a controller must name the actual recipients where it can, not only categories.” § top
    Court of Justice of the European Union 2GDPR 8
  • factArticle 19 of the 2019 Copyright Directive gives authors a yearly right to comprehensive information on the exploitation of their works, including all revenues generated.“up to date, relevant and comprehensive information on the exploitation of their works” § top
    The 2019 Copyright DirectiveThe European Union 19Regulation and law 32
  • factThe platform pays the card fee out of its 15%; its margin is in wallet top-ups, batched settlements and add-ons at a 70% author share, not in the author's share.“The platform's margin is in wallet top-ups, where one card fee covers several downloads, in batching settlements, and in add-ons at a 70% author share” § top
    Pricing and economics 61Micropayments and the reading meter 41

Proposals (5)

  • proposalAn author can send a right-of-access request under GDPR Article 15 asking the ordinary things and two unusual ones: view and download counts per upload, and the recipients of any licensing.“asking the ordinary things and two unusual ones” § top
    GDPR 8Regulation and law 32
  • proposalEvery author gets an encrypted, versioned vault that nobody without a key can read, including the host; a service that cannot read the content cannot sell it or change the terms.“Every author gets a vault.” § top
    The vault 108Encryption and keys 70sgit 91
  • proposalKeys decide access rather than settings: a setting lives on a server and changes with its owner, a key lives with the author and is verified by anyone.“A setting lives on a server and changes when the server's owner changes. A key lives with the author and is verified by anyone.” § top
    Keys, not settingsEncryption and keys 70Identity and secrets 59
  • proposalDecryption happens in the reader's browser; the one bounded exception is an attested enclave that handles one deck's plaintext in memory for one request.“The one bounded exception is the unlock service, an attested enclave that handles one deck's plaintext in memory for one request” § top
    Client-side and on-device 56Encryption and keys 70
  • proposalSeven roles, storage host, key service, unlock service, payments, identity, the front and add-ons, are separated so no party holds more than one of the content, the keys and the money.“The plan separates the roles so that no party holds more than one of the content, the keys and the money” § top
    Keys, not settingsPrivacy and sovereignty 45Supply chains 22

    Dinis Cruz 126GDPR 8

    Deck VaultRisk acceptance 37Startups and early access 34

Limits (5)

    The United Kingdom 19The 2019 Copyright Directive
  • limitA private grant can be revoked, but a file already downloaded is the reader's, and the plan says so on every grant rather than pretending otherwise.“A file already downloaded is the reader's, and the plan says so on every grant rather than pretending otherwise.” § top

  • limitNothing in the build order beyond the vault exists: no unlock enclave, no receipt that becomes a key, no front from a read key alone, no migration tool, and no rail under five pence.“Nothing in the build order beyond the vault exists.” § top
