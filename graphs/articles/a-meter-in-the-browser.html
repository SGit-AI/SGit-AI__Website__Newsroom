Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisThe meter can be escaped but the personalisation cannot be copied; the experiment is whether a site that knows what you read makes a few pence a page feel fair.“The meter can be escaped; the personalisation cannot be copied.” § top

Decisions (3)

  • decisionThe meter has since gone live: it charges by how much of a page you read, the balance can go below zero with nothing blocked, a page can be declined with a reason, and topping up is a £5 Stripe payment.“it charges by how much of a page you read, your balance can go below zero with nothing blocked” § top
    SG Meter 26Stripe 16Scrolled is not read 12
  • decisionThe Portuguese rules are kept: say it is a demonstration, never block a page, charge once per page per visit, and work if the browser refuses to store anything.“never block a page, charge once per page per visit so the back button is not a purchase” § top
