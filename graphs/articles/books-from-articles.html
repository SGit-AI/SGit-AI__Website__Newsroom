Hypotheses (4)

  • hypothesisThe reason is the same every time: a book is one big batch, so when the first version exists every change is expensive, review piles on one person, and when energy moves to the next place of highest leverage the book stays behind.“A book is one big batch. When the first version exists, every change touches everything” § behind
    Books from articles 5Complexity and shipping 60
  • hypothesisWhen an agent like Claude has many of the owner's voice memos and the working history to learn from, it writes material that is authentic and reads well; the articles are exactly that kind of source.“it writes material that is authentic and reads well. The articles on this site are exactly that kind of source.” § articles
    Claude 66The voice memo 96The agents write too 92
  • hypothesisThe hypothesis: a book is a curated, ordered set of finished articles plus the editorial thread that connects them, and the workflow that makes the articles can make the books.“A book becomes a curated, ordered set of finished articles, plus the editorial thread that connects them” § hypothesis
    Books from articles 5The publishing method 42
  • hypothesisThe bigger idea is other people's books: the constraints and failures of a story almost never get captured, and for people like rUv, with 226 public repositories, or Simon Wardley, that can now be captured and made into books.“I think we can now capture that, make it easy to consume, and help make great books from it.” § others
    rUv (Reuven Cohen) 7Simon Wardley 20Books from articles 5
