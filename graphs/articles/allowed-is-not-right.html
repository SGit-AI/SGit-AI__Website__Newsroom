Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisThe evidence for removing the human is the case record: a person's decision becomes precedent P-1, matching cases follow it, a differing case is distinguished and escalates, and a precedent that holds is written into policy and then code.“The evidence for removing the human is the record of the cases where the human was there, and agreed.” § evidence
