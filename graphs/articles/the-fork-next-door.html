Facts (15)

  • factIn an open source company every corner of the triangle, founders, customers and investors, has an exit that takes the technology with it, and each exit has a condition.“In an open source company every corner has an exit, and the exit takes the technology with it.” § triangle
    The fork next doorStartups and early access 34

  • factMariaDB was created in 2009 to protect MySQL from its new owners; Oracle kept MySQL going, and in October 2026 MySQL is second in the DB-Engines ranking and MariaDB thirteenth.“MySQL is second in the DB-Engines ranking, with MariaDB thirteenth” § executing
    MySQLMariaDBOracle 2
  • factThe real defence is execution, and in the generative AI era speed: the moat is a rate, not a wall, and a competitor who forks today gets your position, not your velocity.“the moat is a rate, not a wall: a competitor who forks today gets your position, not your velocity” § executing

  • factThe stronger version of the self-selecting investor is the one who needs the project to succeed because their own business depends on it, as Valkey's and OpenTofu's backers did.“It is the investor who needs the project to succeed, because their own business depends on it.” § investors
    Sell, don't issue 4The fork next door
  • factFrank Karlitschek left ownCloud in April 2016 and Nextcloud launched on a fork on 2 June; it turned profitable by the end of 2016 and ran ten years without outside investment.“turned profitable by the end of 2016” § founders
    NextcloudownCloudFrank Karlitschek
  • factThe founder's exit depends on the contract: venture investors usually ask for a non-compete, and the NVCA's model agreement provides for one from key employees.“those clauses exist to make it expensive for a founder to walk away” § founders
    NVCASell, don't issue 4
  • factOpen code is necessary but not enough for the customers' corner; their real exit is their data in an open format, movable to whoever serves the same code better at a cost close to zero.“The customer's real exit is their data, in an open format, that they can take to whoever serves the same code better, at a cost close to zero.” § customers
    Privacy and sovereignty 45The fork next door
  • factA component many companies depend on can move to a neutral home: Google donated Kubernetes to the newly formed CNCF as it reached version 1.0 in July 2015.“Google donated Kubernetes to the newly formed Cloud Native Computing Foundation as it reached version 1.0 in July 2015” § maturity
    Google 40Kubernetes 3Cloud Native Computing Foundation 4Explorer, villager, town planner 25

    Explorer, villager, town planner 25Simon Wardley 20The fork next door

    Elastic 2Redis 2The fork next door

Proposals (1)

  • proposalA founder who wants the fork to protect them should refuse a broad non-compete and offer narrower promises: confidentiality, no poaching for a while, no use of the brand.“A founder who wants the fork to protect them, not only their investors, should refuse a broad non-compete, and offer narrower promises instead” § founders
    Sell, don't issue 4The fork next door

Limits (11)

  • limitYou can be out-served while still executing: Redis and Elastic changed licences because cloud providers sold their healthy code, and open source does not protect against a larger service provider.“Open source protects the corners of the triangle from each other; it does not protect the company from a larger service provider.” § care
    Redis 2Elastic 2The fork next door
  • limitForks are expensive, so setting up next door is a credible threat for investors who depend on the technology, not for a small angel.“The protection is strongest for the investors who need it most.” § care
    The fork next doorStartups and early access 34
  • limitEvery catalogued relicensing between 2018 and 2024 was by a single corporate copyright holder; the fork protects what is released, and a single holder can still change the terms going forward.“The fork protects what has already been released; a single holder can still change the terms going forward.” § care
    Open source and open licences 53open-source.sgit.ai 9
  • limitNo study tests the triangle as a whole; the cases fit the argument but are not a controlled comparison.“No study tests the triangle as a whole.” § limits
    The fork next door
  • limitThe fork cases are famous ones; successful forks get written about and failed ones mostly do not, which is survivorship bias in its own right.“The fork cases are famous ones.” § limits
    The fork next door
  • limitHigher returns for committed investors are a guess, not a finding; no data shows open source companies return more to investors.“Higher returns are a guess, not a finding.” § limits
    Startups and early access 34
  • limitThe patent figures measure innovation, not financial return, and corporate investors bring their own conflicts.“The patent figures measure innovation, not financial return.” § limits
    Startups and early access 34

    MySQL

    The United Kingdom 19NVCA
