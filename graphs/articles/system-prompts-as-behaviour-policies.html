Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisA system prompt is a behaviour policy written as paragraphs, and it can be written as rows and as a JSON file a harness can check, with stronger controls in more places than the prompt.“Can it be represented as a behaviour policy?” § hypothesis
    Behaviour policy 91Hope or enforcement 65

Proposals (3)

  • proposalAn ontology: one policy per layer, vendor, deployer and user, each with a mandate, a reach, its inputs and their trust, the state that changes rules mid-conversation, and rules with kind, trigger, decision, reason, authority, barrier, enforcement, tests and source text.“So here is the shape I propose, building on the columns this site's policies already use: mandate, reach, gap and barriers” § ontology
    Behaviour policy 91Footprint 22Reach 47
  • proposalThe taxonomy is the eight kinds, each with a natural home; the kind says where a rule could be kept, the barrier says where it is kept today, and the difference is the gap.“The kind says where a rule could be kept; the barrier says where it is kept today; the difference is the gap.” § ontology
    Behaviour policy 91Hooks are kernel code 14
  • proposalThe could_be field turns the policy into a plan for moving rules to a hook, a flag, a signature or a classifier, and a signed, versioned file makes a change to a vendor's rules a diff.“a change to a vendor's rules is a diff, not a re-read of four thousand words” § ontology
    Behaviour policy 91The record 57
