Hypotheses (2)

  • hypothesisOpen source protects the investor too: if investors lose faith in the founder's direction, they can take the code and fund a different team to build a better service on it.“Open source protects the investor too. If the investors lose faith in the founder's direction, they do not need control of the company to act on it” § fork
    The fork as insuranceOpen source and open licences 53
  • hypothesisThe structure does not just suit the founder; it raises the chance that the company succeeds, through money discipline, focus and direction.“this structure does not just suit the founder; it raises the chance that the company succeeds.” § odds

Proposals (5)

  • proposalSell, don't issue: the investor buys shares the founder already owns in a private sale, the money goes to the founder, and the company is not diluted.“In this model, the investor buys shares the founder already owns, in a private sale. The money goes to the founder.” § sell
    Sell, don't issue 4Founder 12
  • proposalLend, don't dilute: if the company needs money, the founder lends some of the proceeds back, subordinated to every other creditor, repaid if the company succeeds, at a modest fixed rate or none.“the founder puts some of the sale proceeds back in, as a loan. The terms are simple: subordinated to every other creditor, repaid if the company succeeds” § lend
    Sell, don't issue 4Founder 12
  • proposalThe honest version writes the subordination in: the founder's loan ranks behind every other creditor by contract and is repaid only from success.“the founder's loan is subordinated by contract, ranking behind every other creditor, and repaid only from success.” § breaks
    Sell, don't issue 4
  • proposalRefuse a broad non-compete, which keeps the fork for the investors and takes it from the founder, and offer narrow protections: confidentiality, no poaching, no use of the brand.“Refuse it, and offer narrow protections instead: confidentiality, no poaching of staff or customers for a period, and no use of the brand.” § breaks
    The fork as insuranceSell, don't issue 4
  • proposalA hybrid keeps most of the benefit: a modest tranche of new ordinary shares that qualify for EIS, a smaller slice of the founder's shares, and a subordinated founder loan for any shortfall.“issue a modest tranche of new ordinary shares that can qualify for EIS, then sell a smaller slice of the founder's shares, and make up any shortfall with a subordinated founder loan.” § fit
    Sell, don't issue 4The Enterprise Investment Scheme
