Definitions (1)

  • definitionA skill behaviour policy looks at three mandates: the described mandate the description promises, the actual mandate from what the files can do, and the grant the environment gives.“three mandates for every skill” § three
    Skill behaviour policyBehaviour policy 91

Decisions (3)

  • decisionEvery finding is recorded as a feature, not a verdict, because an upload can be exfiltration or a login and only the context decides.“The important design choice is that every finding is a feature, not a verdict.” § features
