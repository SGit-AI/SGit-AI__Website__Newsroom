Hypotheses (1)

Definitions (2)

  • definitionRead through the Agent Behaviour Policy, the desktop is the agent's grant, the task is its mandate, and the desktop's job is to keep the first close to the second and leave a readable footprint.“the desktop is the agent's grant: everything it can reach. The task is its mandate.” § top
    Behaviour policy 91Reach 47Footprint 22RiskMandate 97
  • definitionA locked-down agent desktop has nine properties: isolation, an egress allowlist, secrets kept outside, an identity of its own, a live view and takeover, a record, a clean reset, billing that follows the work, and the right operating system.“The first seven are about safety, the eighth about whether it is affordable to do it properly, and the ninth about whether it can be done at all.” § top
