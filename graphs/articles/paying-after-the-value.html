Hypotheses (3)

  • hypothesisAlmost every purchase is a bet on future value: a subscription, a gym membership or a one-off purchase is a bet that it will be used enough for the price of each use to fall to an acceptable number.“Almost every time I buy something, I am buying future value.” § future
    Pricing and economics 61
  • hypothesisThe point of the negative balance is psychological, not financial: by the time a reader is below zero they have read dozens of articles and the request to pay is connected to them.“The point of the negative balance is psychological, not financial.” § meter
    The pain of payingMicropayments and the reading meter 41
  • hypothesisA once-a-year request feels heavy because the value is thousands of small moments nobody adds up: the tragedy of the commons applied to your own reading.“a once-a-year request feels heavy, because the value is thousands of small moments that nobody adds up” § wikipedia
    Wikipedia 4The pain of paying

Definitions (1)

  • definitionThe pain of paying, in Prelec and Loewenstein's 1998 model, plays an important role in consumer self-regulation but is hedonically costly, and consumption already paid for can be enjoyed as if free.“The pain of paying, we argue, plays an important role in consumer self-regulation, but is hedonically costly.” § psychology
    The pain of payingDrazen PrelecGeorge Loewenstein
