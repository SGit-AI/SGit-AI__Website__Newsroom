Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisNow the first review happens before publishing, against the record rather than the crowd; it does not replace readers, it means what they read already has its evidence attached, every link, a section on what the article does not claim, and where it came from.“Now the first review happens before publishing, against the record rather than against the crowd.” § peer-review
