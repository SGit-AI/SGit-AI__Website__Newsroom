Facts (12)

  • factAgents almost always come from a session: a prompt that worked, wrapped in a workflow, sent to production because it worked, which is the explorer side of the map, the right place to build and the wrong place to run at scale.“That is the explorer side of the map, genesis and custom-built, and it is the right place to build something new.” § problem

  • factWhat agents run on is commodity billed by use: the invoice exact, the logs complete, the traces written whether asked or not, nobody reading them together, and the owner of cost, the node that should connect bill to agents, missing.“the one node on the map that should connect the bill to the agents, an owner of cost, is missing” § problem
    Wardley maps 24Cost is an engineering problem
  • factAn agent that retries in a loop is a bug, a workflow that sends its whole context on every call is a bug, and a key with no ceiling is a missing control; the service exists to make them visible.“An agent that retries in a loop is a bug.” § problem

Hypotheses (2)

  • hypothesisIt is a business rather than a consulting engagement because the inputs exist in every company with agents in production, nobody reads them together, and the two outputs, a shorter bill and a map of what to build, are things companies pay for.“The reason I think it is a business, and not a consulting engagement” § top
    Startups and early access 34Cost is an engineering problem
  • hypothesisA cost review is bought once and resented, a map of what to build is returned to, so the service sells cost reduction and delivers engineering opportunity, and the business model follows the second.“So the service sells cost reduction, and delivers engineering opportunity, and the business model follows the second.” § positive
    Strategy and positioning 26Cost is an engineering problem

Proposals (2)

  • proposalA business plan to be handed to whoever wants to build it: a service that helps companies manage the cost of agentic AI by observing what is already happening in their estate.“help companies manage the cost of agentic AI, by observing what is already happening” § top
    Startups and early access 34RiskMandate 97Cost is an engineering problem
  • proposalMove: anonymised extracts leave as sealed envelopes to a benchmark vault, seen by the customer before they go, and over time that vault lets the service say what companies like yours spend, which is the second business.“Over time that vault is the thing that lets the service say” § vaults
    Encryption and keys 70Startups and early access 34The vault 108

Definitions (2)

  • definitionCost is an engineering problem: an unexplained agent bill is an estate that shipped from the explorer side without the villager's discipline, a class of defect with a cause, a fix and a test, not a discount to negotiate.“not a thing you fix by negotiating a discount, but a class of defect with a cause, a fix and a test” § problem
    Cost is an engineering problemExplorer, villager, town planner 25
  • definitionThe twin of the estate is a graph of agents, models, keys, tools, data, owners and schedules with a cost on every edge, which can be queried, compared with the invoice and followed; the connector twin is one component of it.“a graph of agents, models, keys, tools, data, owners and schedules, with a cost on every edge” § service
    Connectors and twins 25Cost is an engineering problem

Decisions (1)

  • decisionThe service installs nothing: the customer drops invoices by key and day, logs and traces, prompts, workflow files and schedules into a vault they own, sealed to the service's key.“No agent is installed and nothing in the customer's environment is changed.” § service
    The vault 108Encryption and keys 70sgit 91

Limits (5)

  • limitWhat does not exist: the catalogue of cost analyses, the twin schema for an agent estate, the log parsers for common platforms, the benchmark vault, and a single customer; a few weeks of work.“That is the work, and it is a few weeks of it, which is why I am writing the plan rather than keeping it.” § exists
    Startups and early access 34Dinis Cruz 126
  • limitNo customer has been read; the method is assembled from pieces that run elsewhere, and the two lists are what the owner expects to find, not what has been found.“No customer has been read.” § limits
