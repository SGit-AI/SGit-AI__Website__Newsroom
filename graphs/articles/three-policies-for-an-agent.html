Definitions (3)

  • definitionAn Agent Decision Policy says, at a branch point, who chooses, on what evidence, within what limits, and who is called when out of bounds; it holds the decider, evidence, envelope, escalation, kill switch, sign-off and record.“The decider, the evidence, the envelope (budget, blast radius, reversibility), escalation, a kill switch, the sign-off and the record” § three
    Agent decision policy 9Blast radius 36
  • definitionAn Agent Workflow Policy says which steps run in what order, and holds states, transitions and the gates between them.“States, transitions, and the gates between them” § three
    Agent workflow policy 7
  • definitionEvery element of all three policies carries five states, kept separately: defined, implemented, enforced, observed and verified.“So I would give every element five states, kept separately” § states
