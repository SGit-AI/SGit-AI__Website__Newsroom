Hypotheses (2)

  • hypothesisAgent behaviour is becoming an existential threat to the model companies, through liability and above all through trust: a decent number of people badly hurt is enough for many more to stop giving agents instructions.“I think agent behaviour is becoming an existential threat to the companies that make the models” § existential
    The liability line 16Incidents and resilience 25A policy beside the prompt
  • hypothesisA system prompt is a light behaviour policy already and should be written as one, layered: the vendor's published policy, then the deployer's with the business rules only the deployer can write.“a system prompt is a light version of one already: it tells the agent how to behave” § system-prompt
    Behaviour policy 91A policy beside the prompt

Proposals (4)

  • proposalBeside the prompt and the vendor's system prompt, an agent should take an Agent Behaviour Policy written to a published standard, which the vendor accepts, enforces as far as it can, and eventually answers for.“It should also get an Agent Behaviour Policy, written to a published standard, which the vendor accepts, enforces as far as it can, and eventually answers for.” § top
    A policy beside the promptBehaviour policy 91
  • proposalThe split: the rows the vendor accepted, the vendor keeps and answers for if they break; everything the policy does not cover stays with the user, and the vendors that keep more rows as boundaries can sell that as the product.“The rows the vendor accepted, the vendor keeps, and answers for if they break.” § inside
    A policy beside the promptThe liability line 16Hope or enforcement 65
  • proposalThe standard needs five things: a versioned signed policy file, the vendor's acknowledgement per row, the vendor's own policy in the same format, a same-day breach report, and liability per row starting with the boundaries.“A versioned policy file, with the reach, the mandate, the gap and the barriers, row by row, signed by the deployer” § inside
    A policy beside the promptBehaviour policy 91
  • proposalReverse-engineer the policies agents already run, as the Gmail connector's thirty tools were measured, and publish them in the ABP format; the gap between measured and published is a finding.“If the vendors will not publish their policies yet, the measured ones are the next best thing, and the gap between the two is a finding.” § already
    abp.sgit.ai 23Gmail 17Behaviour policy 91
