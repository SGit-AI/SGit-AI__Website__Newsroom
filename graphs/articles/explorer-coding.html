Facts (17)

  • factThe term vibe coding is Andrej Karpathy's, from a post in February 2025 about throwaway weekend projects, and by November it was Collins Dictionary's word of the year.“He was describing throwaway weekend projects, and by November it was Collins Dictionary's word of the year.” § name
    The citizen developer 13Andrej Karpathy 4
  • factAndrew Ng said in May 2025 that it is unfortunate the practice is called vibe coding, and that coding with AI is a deeply intellectual exercise.“It's unfortunate that that's called vibe coding” § name
    The citizen developer 13Andrew Ng
  • factExplorer is Simon Wardley's word for people who explore the uncharted land, who show you wonder but fail a lot, which is what someone building their own tool with an agent does.“They show you wonder but they fail a lot.” § name
    Explorer, villager, town planner 25Wardley maps 24Simon Wardley 20
  • factThe owner could not find anyone using explorer coding as a term; the nearest is exploratory programming, the research term for programming while requirements are still being discovered.“I could not find anyone using explorer coding as a term.” § name
    Explorer, villager, town planner 25
  • factSpreadsheets won because of the feedback loop, change a cell and see the result, and what has changed is that the loop is now fast for anything.“Spreadsheets won because of the feedback loop” § spreadsheet
    The citizen developer 13
  • factWhen someone in HR explored what her team would build and the agents they would manage, her answer was Amazing! When can I start?“her answer was: "Amazing! When can I start?"” § spreadsheet
    The citizen developer 13
  • factWhat explorers produce is the best brief there is: a working app says what its user wants better than any document, because it runs.“A working app says what its user wants better than any document, because it runs.” § spreadsheet
    The brief 45Explorer, villager, town planner 25
  • factAn explorer-coded app may look and feel like an application, but nobody has read the code or validated the architecture, and it is not ready to be in line.“Nobody has read the code. Nobody has validated the architecture.” § in-line
    Explorer, villager, town planner 25Code review 18
  • factDependence comes quickly: once something gets used it is mission critical, even for one person, and from then on it has to be maintained and kept working.“Once something gets used, it is mission critical, because people depend on it” § in-line
    Explorer, villager, town planner 25
  • factVillagers take what the explorer built and make it safe to depend on: maintenance, tests, releases that do not break it, agent behaviour policies and rules for the data the agents touch.“That is the villagers' work.” § handover
    Explorer, villager, town planner 25Behaviour policy 91
  • factThe Linux kernel's rules say AI agents must not add Signed-off-by tags, only humans can certify the Developer Certificate of Origin, and the submitter takes full responsibility.“AI agents MUST NOT add Signed-off-by tags” § handover
    The Linux kernel 2Explorer, villager, town planner 25
  • factFedora's policy proposal says the contributor is always the author and fully accountable for their contributions.“The contributor is always the author and is fully accountable for their contributions.” § handover
    FedoraExplorer, villager, town planner 25
  • factAn explorer cannot be made accountable for code they never read; the team the work is handed to can, and the town planners above all.“We cannot make an explorer accountable for code they never read.” § handover
    Explorer, villager, town planner 25Code review 18
  • factThe further towards commodity the code sits on the map, the fewer agents in line and the more deterministic code, as argued for code review and for cost.“the further towards commodity, the fewer agents in line, and the more deterministic code” § handover
    Wardley maps 24Code review 18
  • factEach conversion step is a revenue stream, and each submission tells the vendor exactly what its users want.“Each step is a revenue stream, and each submission tells the vendor exactly what its users want.” § products
    Pricing and economics 61The brief 45
  • factThe same path should exist inside companies and around open-source projects; the custom Firefox plan walks through it for one product.“The custom Firefox plan walks through what it would look like for one product.” § products
    Open source and open licences 53Firefox 9
  • factThe article comes from a voice memo of 16 October 2026, written by the site's agent in the site's voice, with the sources read the same day.“A voice memo of mine on 16 October 2026, written by this site's agent in the voice of this site” § sources
    The voice memo 96

Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisThe owner thinks this is the best thing to happen to software since the spreadsheet, an explosion of empowerment.“That is why I think it is the best thing to happen to software since the spreadsheet” § spreadsheet
    The citizen developer 13Explorer, villager, town planner 25

Proposals (2)

  • proposalThe owner proposes explorer coding as a better name for what is usually called vibe coding.“I would like to propose a better name for what is usually called vibe coding: explorer coding.” § top
    Explorer, villager, town planner 25The citizen developer 13
  • proposalEvery product needs an explorer version, in which users talk to the product with a model, reshape it and keep their own version, with paid conversions to a villager product and a town planner one.“Products need to become explorer-friendly; more than that, they need an explorer version.” § products
    Explorer, villager, town planner 25Pricing and economics 61Custom interfaces 28
