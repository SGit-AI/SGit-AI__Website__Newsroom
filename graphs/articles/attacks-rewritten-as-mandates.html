Hypotheses (2)

  • hypothesisRead with those four columns, every row of the ledger is the same shape: a credential, a tag or a build step could do far more than it was meant to, and the attacker used the difference, reach beyond mandate.“By my reading, the other rows have the same shape.” § reach
