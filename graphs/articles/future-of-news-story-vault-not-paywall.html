Definitions (2)

  • definitionThe story is a graph of claims, evidence, sources, raw materials, analysis and drafts; the article is one projection of it, a walk through the graph for one audience at one moment.“the story is a graph; the article is a projection.” § top
    Story vaults and liquid content 15Fractal semantic graphs 62The SGit Newsroom 72
  • definitionEvidence is frozen bytes with a SHA-256 hash; provenance is the path from a claim through named edges to that evidence, a statement of what the claim rests on, not that it is true; trust is earned when provenance holds over time.“Provenance is not a statement that the claim is true. It is a statement of what the claim rests on” § top
