Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisWhen a person in the loop has no agency, responsibility should sit with whoever designed the decision point, their boss and their boss's boss, who authorised a system that will statistically make mistakes.“It should sit with whoever designed the decision point, and their boss, and their boss's boss” § top

Proposals (3)

  • proposalThe final decision should not be an agent's but code, a decision tree of checks each of which can be followed and questioned; models prepare and propose, rules decide, people own the rules.“Models prepare and propose; rules decide; people own the rules.” § top
    Agent decision policy 9Behaviour policy 91The agency scale 10
  • proposalAs drafts of one kind keep coming back correct, the behaviour policy changes to allow that agent in that setup to send that kind of email and nothing else, so the proven cases become rules.“the behaviour policy changes to allow that agent, in that setup, to send that kind of email, and nothing else” § top
    Behaviour policy 91The agency scale 10
  • proposalWhere to start: list the decision points, find the weakest dimension of each, replace identifiers with things, make why answerable, check the incentives, move accountability below level 3, treat review as QA, and turn clean passes into rules.“Find the weakest dimension of each. That is the only one worth fixing first.” § top

Definitions (2)

  • definitionAgency has seven dimensions, options, context, depth, time, incentives, escalation and authority, each observable at the decision point, and the weakest one caps the decision.“options, context, depth, time, incentives, escalation and authority” § top
    The agency scale 10
  • definitionAbove level 3 a review is QA, not a sign-off: the useful question is how we got here, and the right fix is to the record, the graph or the objectives rather than the email.“The right fix is not to the email. It is to the record, the graph, the connection” § top
