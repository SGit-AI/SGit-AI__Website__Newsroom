Definitions (5)

  • definitionThe ABP's four words: reach, what the agent can do, measured; mandate, what the business authorised, written down; gap, reach minus mandate, derived; barriers, what stands in the way of each row of the gap.“Gap: reach minus mandate, derived, never written by hand.” § lineage
