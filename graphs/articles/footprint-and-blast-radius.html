Definitions (5)

  • definitionA barrier is typed as a boundary, a setting, an expectation or nothing; only the boundary is a control, and most of what organisations call controls turn out to be expectations once typed.“Only the boundary is a control, and most of what organisations call controls turn out to be expectations once they are typed.” § top
    Hope or enforcement 65Behaviour policy 91
  • definitionThe footprint is the set of things the agent actually did, in a period, read from the record afterwards; it is evidence, not a decision.“The footprint is the set of things the agent actually did, in a period, read from the record afterwards.” § top
    Footprint 22
  • definitionFootprint in the gap, an action within the reach and outside the mandate that nothing stopped, is a near miss, the most valuable finding because it arrives before the damage.“In the safety disciplines this is a near miss, and it is the most valuable kind of finding there is, because it arrives before the damage.” § top
    The near miss 3Incidents and resilience 25Footprint 22
  • definitionA dormant mandate is a row the owner asked for that the footprint never shows: the mandate was over-stated, a check never ran, or the agent cannot do it.“A row the owner asked for that the footprint never shows.” § top
    Dormant mandateFootprint 22
  • definitionBlast radius is not a sixth set but a measure attached to a row: what it would cost the business if that row were used in full, today.“what it would cost the business if that row were used in full, today” § top
