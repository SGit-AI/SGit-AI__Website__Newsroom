Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisThe reliable money comes from companies paying for a customised build at a price well below what it costs them to keep the tool working themselves, which is how RiskMandate and sgit already work.“The reliable money comes from companies, paying for a customised build at a price well below what it costs them to keep the tool working themselves.” § says
    Pricing and economics 61Market and competitors 21RiskMandate 97sgit 91

Proposals (1)

  • proposalWhat is missing is the plumbing: one annual charge that covers many projects, payment tied to a verified build rather than a count of installs, and a split of the money between maintainers and local support firms.“one annual charge that covers many projects, payment tied to a verified build rather than a count of installs” § says
