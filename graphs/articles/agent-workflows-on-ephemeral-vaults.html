  • factMost agents on this site already work this way: the wire agent reads vaults research desks write holding only the read key, isolated agents keep memory in a vault, and a vault is the shared file system, channel and record.“a vault is the shared file system, the message channel and the record, and its keys decide who can do what” § pattern

  • factMany of these vaults are ephemeral: when the work is done they can be deleted from the server or left as ciphertext nobody holds the key to.“or simply left as ciphertext nobody holds the key to” § pattern

  • factThe seven architectures run from one agent with one ephemeral vault, through writer and readers, fan-out by folder, append-only workers, no network, and watched, to a swarm of a hundred; a scoped clone is a speed setting, the boundary is the key.“a scoped clone is a speed setting, not a boundary: the boundary is the key” § architectures

Data points (5)

  • data-pointWith in-memory storage, a hundred writers posting ten entries each landed all 1,000 entries in an append lane in 4.9 seconds, about 205 a second, and every accepted entry was listed afterwards. 1,000 entries landed in 4.9 seconds by a hundred writers“A hundred writers posting ten entries each landed all 1,000 in 4.9 seconds, about 205 a second.” § swarm
    Vault design 48sgit 91
  • data-pointMeasured with sgit 0.21.0 and vault server 0.34.0 as one process on four cores, 1, 10, 50 and 100 writers each posting one 2 KB entry took 0.004, 0.03, 0.12 and 0.27 seconds. 0.27 seconds for 100 writers posting one entry each“took 0.004, 0.03, 0.12 and 0.27 seconds” § swarm
    Vault design 48sgit 91
  • data-pointA hundred writers sharing one token sent 1,200 entries; exactly 1,000 were accepted and 200 refused at the server's cap of 1,000 waiting entries per inbox, the case for one token per worker. 200 of 1,200 entries refused at the inbox cap“exactly 1,000 were accepted and 200 refused at the server's cap of 1,000 waiting entries per inbox” § swarm
    Vault design 48Encryption and keys 70
  • data-pointTen scoped clones of one folder of a 2,000-file vault at once took 1.9 seconds with the server mostly idle; ten full clones of a 500-file vault took 21.1, on one core of a single process. 21.1 seconds for ten full clones, against 1.9 for ten scoped clones“Ten full clones of a 500-file vault took 21.1” § swarm
    Vault design 48
  • data-pointA scoped clone barely grows with the vault, 0.59 seconds at 100 files and 0.77 at 2,000, while a full clone went from 0.95 to 5.55. 0.77 seconds for a scoped clone at 2,000 files“0.59 seconds at 100 files, 0.77 at 2,000, while a full clone went from 0.95 to 5.55” § swarm

Proposals (4)

  • proposalCapture the pattern as a series of architectures from simple to complex, each in a repository of its own with its agents, keys, fixtures and a CI job that proves it works, forkable as templates, recipes and skills.“each in a repository of its own with its agents, its keys, its fixtures and a CI job that proves it works” § top
    The ephemeral vaultComplexity and shipping 60Agent tooling 32
  • proposalThe repository should run as one of the checks before anything reaches sgit's main branch.“The repository should run as one of the checks before anything reaches sgit's main branch.” § top
    sgit 91Complexity and shipping 60
  • proposalA vault app gives the visual representation people ask for: a page that lives in the vault draws its contents, a screenshot is stored back in the vault, and the vault's key stays in the host.“open the vault, the app draws its contents, take a screenshot, store it in the vault, and carry on” § watched
    Custom interfaces 28The vault 108
  • proposalThe brief in eight items: a folder per architecture, a CI job each, three environments, a vault app each, the swarm test as a repeatable job, the defects as issues, the gate before sgit's main branch, and templates.“The repository runs before a release reaches sgit's main branch; a failure blocks it.” § brief
