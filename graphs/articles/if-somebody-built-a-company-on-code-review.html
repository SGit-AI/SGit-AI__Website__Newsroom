Proposals (12)

  • proposalUse the models to build, prune and maintain the graphs against reality and then take them out of the line, so what runs on every change is graphs, deterministic checks and formulas.“A model in the line on every change is the art again with a different reviewer.” § top
    Code review 18Fractal semantic graphs 62
  • proposalA review company should embrace the differences between teams: the generic parts are the grammar, the tools that work on any graph in it, and a few workflows; the layers are the customer's.“A code review product that tries to standardise the layers away loses the thing it was meant to review.” § top
    Code review 18Strategy and positioning 26
  • proposalProject a graph downward from the stories before the code exists and derive one upward from the syntax tree when it does; the review is the join, with three outcomes: match, derived not projected, projected not derived.“The review is the join between those two graphs, and the join has exactly three outcomes” § top
    Projected and derived graphsCode review 18
  • proposalStories should be rules and examples, after Example Mapping: a story is a small tree whose leaves a test or a run can satisfy, and the question cards are the join's findings before code exists.“a leaf is a thing a test or a run can satisfy” § top

  • proposalReview is not saying no, it is sorting: split a change by what it can reach, merge what provably reaches no behaviour by a deterministic check, and give the fifty lines on a critical path to people with context.“Review is sorting what can go fast from what must go slow” § top
    Reach 47Blast radius 36Code review 18The OWASP O2 Platform 5
