Definitions (4)

  • definitionA new dimension is needed where the ontology and taxonomy change; a layer with the same vocabulary further away is the same level seen from further away.“The moment the ontology and taxonomy change, that is where a new dimension is needed.” § memo
    Fractal semantic graphs 62
  • definitionThe grammar is five rules: every edge a verb with a named inverse, no generic association edge, properties carry data not meaning, supersede never delete, never render the whole graph.“The grammar is graphs.sgit.ai's and the fractal graphs article's, and it is five rules.” § grammar
