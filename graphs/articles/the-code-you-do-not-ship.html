Hypotheses (2)

  • hypothesisThe direction of travel is the minimal viable code path, and it applies at every level: company, product, feature and code.“So I think the direction of travel is clear, and it applies at every level.” § path
    The code you do not ship 18
  • hypothesisNone of this will happen because it is more secure; minimal code has to win on what people feel: faster, lighter, simpler software.“None of this will happen because it is more secure.” § driver
    The code you do not ship 18Complexity and shipping 60

Proposals (2)

  • proposalThe specialists who know a component best maintain a minimal version of it for the people who use it, and get paid for that rather than for new features.“maintain a minimal version of it for the people who use it, and get paid for that, not for new features” § market
    A fractal market of maintainersPricing and economics 61
  • proposalIn the fractal market each piece ships with its own behaviour, decision and workflow policies and its Wardley stage, explorer, villager or town planner, so a buyer can choose by risk.“Each piece ships with its own behaviour policy, what the code can reach; its own decision policy, where it decides; and its own workflow policy, the flows it runs.” § market
    A fractal market of maintainersBehaviour policy 91Explorer, villager, town planner 25Simon Wardley 20
