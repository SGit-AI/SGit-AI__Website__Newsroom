Hypotheses (4)

  • hypothesisThe likely reason is an in-app web view on iOS, which Apple does not allow to use passkeys for services outside the app's associated domains.“The likely reason is less mysterious than it felt.” § happened
    Apple 21WebAuthn and passkeys 15Identity and secrets 59
  • hypothesisUsers tolerate complex workflows once, when they can see the value and the steps are not repeated; WhatsApp's linked devices and Midjourney's Discord bot are the examples.“Users have a lot of tolerance for complex workflows when they can see the value, when the steps are not repeated, and when they are connected to the value.” § hard
    Identity and secrets 59WhatsApp 10Midjourney
  • hypothesisIf the connector's limit is for security it buys little, because an agent that wants to leak can share a Drive folder or send fifty small emails, and it pushes people to other tools, which is the bigger risk.“If it is security, I do not think it buys much” § email
    Connectors and twins 25Agents and email 19
  • hypothesisA wire for one community or for the customers of one platform is a product of its own, and the value proposition comes from almost no new code.“If we can add value there, that is a very good value proposition, from almost no new code.” § wire
    The industry wire 10The code you do not ship 18Pricing and economics 61

Proposals (4)

  • proposalEvery reader vault should have an append lane that any browser can write to and none can read, offered as a URL, as text and as a QR code carrying only the lane address and the public key.“So the vault pane should offer it three ways: a URL I can open, the text I can copy and paste, and a QR code I can scan from the other device.” § request
    Vault design 48Encryption and keys 70The SGit Newsroom 72
  • proposalThe brief has six steps: a reader key pair, an append lane on the reader vault, the link three ways, the sender's side, the drain on unlock, and tests.“Only the reader's key pair and the pane's link are new.” § brief
    The brief 45Vault design 48Encryption and keys 70
  • proposalBecause anyone holding the link can write to the lane, the token should be rotatable, the drain should show what arrived before merging, and a link should be able to expire.“anyone holding the link can write to the lane, so the token should be rotatable from the pane” § brief
    Vault design 48Encryption and keys 70
  • proposalWhether readers accept the one-off step is something to test, not assume: A/B test the link against a pairing flow and see which readers finish.“We can A/B test the link against a pairing flow and see which readers finish.” § hard
    Synthetic users 26Vault design 48
