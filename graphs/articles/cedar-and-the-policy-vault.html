Definitions (2)

  • definitionCedar in five ideas: default deny, forbid wins over permit, four parts to every request, principal, action, resource and context, a schema with a validator, and proof in Lean as well as tests.“even if a permit policy is satisfied, any satisfied forbid policy overrides it, producing a Deny decision.” § language
    Cedar 7Policy as code 3
  • definitionThe crosswalk: the grant is a permit and the schema's actions, a barrier is a forbid, the footprint is the entities, a skill row is a forbid on labels, and business layers are forbids on facts passed as context.“Permit is the grant, forbid is the barrier, the schema is the footprint.” § crosswalk
    Their terms, our termsBehaviour policy 91Footprint 22Cedar 7

Decisions (3)

  • decisionCedar is the policy language to adopt rather than compete with: the aim is for agent behaviour policies to connect to what others already use, not to sit beside them as one more format.“I want our agent behaviour policies to connect to what others are already using, not to sit beside them as one more format.” § why
    Cedar 7Behaviour policy 91Policy as code 3
  • decisionEdges between Cedar's world and the behaviour policy's go in Cedar annotations: every demo policy carries one back to its ABP rule, skill row or layer, a fractal semantic graph move done in code.“We do not need to replace either. We need edges between them, and Cedar gives us a place to put them: annotations.” § crosswalk
    Fractal semantic graphs 62Their terms, our termsCedar 7
  • decisionThe site's agent built the demo on a local vault server with sgit 0.21.0 and cedarpy 4.12.2: a schema, four policy files with seven policies, the entities and the test cases, each a request with its expected decision.“this site's agent built it on a local vault server, with sgit 0.21.0 and cedarpy 4.12.2.” § demo
    sgit 91Cedar 7The policy vaultThe vault 108
