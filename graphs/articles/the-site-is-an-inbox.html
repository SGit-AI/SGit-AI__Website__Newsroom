Hypotheses (4)

  • hypothesisThe brief could not have been written two days earlier, not for lack of time or ideas but for lack of constraints: the properties of what runs are what let the author think.“The properties are what let me think.” § short
    The constraints are what let me think 2Dinis Cruz 126
  • hypothesisThe feedback loop is also build it, learn its properties, and only then have the next idea, because the next idea is made of the properties; ship first, think second.“Ship first, think second, and do not pretend the thinking could have come first.” § constraints
    The constraints are what let me think 2Complexity and shipping 60
  • hypothesisThe economics: the more a reader marks the more they use the site and pay by the meter, and the more their graph is worth; the loop closes only if reading with a graph that grows is better than reading without one.“reading here, with a graph that grows, is a better experience than reading without one” § brief

Proposals (5)

  • proposalTwo views on demand: every term in the piece as a link into the vocabulary, and the piece classified sentence by sentence as the five readers see it, with the reader's disagreement recorded as a mark.“the reader should see that view and be able to disagree with it, sentence by sentence, and the disagreement is a mark” § brief
    The reader's own graphThe agents write too 92
  • proposalPersonas should be disposable, each with its own key pair in the master vault, one to sign and one to receive, so a persona can be handed to an agent as a read key to its slice of the graph only.“Personas should be disposable, and each should have a key pair of its own” § brief
    Personas and personalisation 33Identity and secrets 59CISO 11Founder 12
  • proposalA mark addressed to the newsroom goes down the write-only lane encrypted to the newsroom's key, signed by the persona's key, with a copy kept in the reader's vault, and the reply is a new version of the article.“It is signed by the persona's key, so the newsroom knows which of my personas said it and that it was me.” § brief

  • proposalAn experiment per feature: each is small enough to ship in two versions and compare with a synthetic reader, two variants, a measurement, and the one that holds.“a synthetic reader, two variants, a measurement, and the one that holds” § brief

Definitions (3)

  • definitionThe site is an inbox that starts empty: every piece, an article it wrote or a wire story, waits for a decision, and the state the site should always be able to return to is empty.“The state the site should always be able to return to is empty.” § brief
    The site is an inbox 2The SGit Newsroom 72The industry wire 10
  • definitionA decision at the end of a page is gamification, a signal the site engineered which is then not a signal; a decision is offered at the moment the reader would take it.“a signal we engineered, which is then not a signal” § brief
    No gamificationScrolled is not read 12
  • definitionA mark is a node carrying the phrase, the article and its version, the persona, the kind and the time; a comment is a node with an edge to its phrase; an inbox decision is an edge from persona to piece.“A mark is a node. It carries the phrase, the article and its version, the persona that made it, the kind, and the time.” § brief
    The reader's own graphFractal semantic graphs 62Personas and personalisation 33
