Hypotheses (6)

Decisions (6)

  • decisionThe meter goes live: it charges by how far down a page you scroll, lets the balance go below zero forever, shows one small balance in the top bar, and lets you refuse to pay with a reason.“It charges by how far down a page you scroll, it lets your balance go below zero forever” § top
    SG Meter 26Micropayments and the reading meter 41sgit.ai 62
  • decisionAt the foot of an article, four reasons not to be charged; the charge is refunded and the reason kept in the reader's history as a signal that shapes their picks.“The charge is refunded, and the reason is kept in your history as a signal.” § top
    SG Meter 26Personas and personalisation 33
  • decisionThe meter is rebuilt as SG Meter, a web component another site can add with a script tag and a config.“so another site can add it with a script tag and a config” § top
    SG Meter 26sgit.ai 62
  • decisionAdded the same day in v0.7.34, the reader sets part of the price: three scales at the foot, where usefulness of 1 is free, 3 the price and 5 double.“A page is now charged only as much as you read it and only as much as you say it was worth.” § top
