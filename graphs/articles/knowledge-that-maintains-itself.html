Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisNobody builds a correct graph of an organisation on the first try, and projects that try spend a year on the ontology and never ship; the graph does not have to be right, it has to be maintained, and the loop maintains it.“The graph does not have to be right. It has to be maintained, and the loop maintains it.” § maintain

Proposals (3)

  • proposalBuild a fractal semantic graph of an organisation's policies and internal knowledge: an ontology and a taxonomy for every element, each part in the vocabulary of whoever owns it, with named edges whose verbs read both ways.“each part kept in the vocabulary of whoever owns it, and named edges between them, with verbs that read both ways” § idea
    Knowledge that maintains itself 5Fractal semantic graphs 62
  • proposalBrief people and agents from the graph, not the pile of documents: a person joining a team or an agent starting a task needs the part of the graph around what they are about to do and what changed since they last looked.“They need the part of the graph around what they are about to do, and what changed since they last looked.” § idea
    Knowledge that maintains itself 5The brief 45Memory and context 46
