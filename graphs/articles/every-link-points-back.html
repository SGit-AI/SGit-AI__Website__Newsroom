Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisStatus since, a plan in an older article that has or has not happened, is probably the most common kind of correction, and the kind of drift that turns into a quoted claim.“The quieter kind, and I think the most common.” § corrections
    Corrections must propagate 47

Proposals (4)

  • proposalEvery article should carry four kinds of forward link: What came next, Corrected by and Status since at the top, and Cited by at the foot.“What I would like, for every article, is to be able to see what came after it” § proposal
    Corrections must propagate 47The publishing method 42
  • proposalWhat came next is added by the release that publishes the later article, in the same commit.“Added by the release that publishes the later article, as part of the same commit.” § proposal
    Corrections must propagate 47The publishing method 42
  • proposalCross-article links could carry a relation, continues, corrects or cites, so a validator could warn when a correction is not acknowledged by the earlier article.“so a validator could warn when a later article says it corrects an earlier one and the earlier one does not say so” § proposal
    Corrections must propagate 47Fractal semantic graphs 62
  • proposalForward links should be added in turns from the most recent articles down, a few at a time, with the counts above as the baseline.“Start from the most recent articles and go down the line, a few at a time, taking turns with the new writing.” § proposal
    Corrections must propagate 47
