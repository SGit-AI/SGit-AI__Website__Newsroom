Definitions (1)

  • definitionA twin, in the sense meant here, is a copy of a process detailed enough to run decisions through before they are made in the real one.“A twin, in the sense I mean, is a copy of a process detailed enough to run decisions through before they are made in the real one.” § twin
    Connectors and twins 25

Decisions (2)

  • decisionThe twin is one app, index.html, and one engine, engine.js, over one content file, content.json, which holds the customers, fourteen orders, thirteen cases, five rules, ten controls and four designs.“The twin is one app, index.html, and one engine, engine.js, over one content file, content.json.” § built
    The Northstar twin 5Client-side and on-device 56
  • decisionThe engine is deterministic: no model is called and the agent's mistakes are scripted per case, so a run replays exactly and anyone can check it.“It is deterministic: no model is called, and the agent's mistakes are scripted per case. That is a choice.” § built
    The Northstar twin 5Synthetic users 26
