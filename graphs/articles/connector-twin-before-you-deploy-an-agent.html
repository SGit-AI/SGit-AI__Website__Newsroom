The article: Before you give an agent a connector, give the connector a twin (2026-09-24). Every claim below is the article's own, in plain words, with the sentence it rests on and a link to its section.

Facts (19)

  • factIf you cannot say what your agent did, what it saw when it did it, and which of its actions you can undo, you are not ready to give it a connector.“If you cannot say what your agent did, what it saw when it did it, and which of its actions you can undo, you are not ready to give it a connector.” § top
    Connectors and twins 25Behaviour policy 91
  • factA connector is a grant of action, not just of access: an agent with Gmail and Calendar connectors can send, trash, permanently delete, move events and cancel meetings at machine speed.“A connector is a grant of action, not just of access.” § top
    Connectors and twins 25Reach 47Gmail 17Google Workspace 10
  • factThe Gmail API documents its delete as immediate and permanent: this operation cannot be undone, prefer messages.trash instead.“Immediately and permanently deletes the specified message. This operation cannot be undone. Prefer messages.trash instead.” § top
    One-way door 18Gmail 17
  • factAn edited calendar event has no version history a user can open; administrators have an audit log with some earlier values.“No version history a user can open.” § top
    One-way door 18Google Workspace 10
  • factGoogle Vault searches Calendar since November 2023 but says it is not designed to be a backup or archive tool and has no automated recovery tools.“Vault isn't designed to be a backup or archive tool” § top
    Google VaultGoogle 40
  • factNone of the platform's records holds what the agent was looking at when it acted or the instruction it was following.“None of these records holds what the agent was looking at when it acted, or the instruction it was following.” § top

  • factCapture copies the tool call, the request and the response of every connector call, and the instruction behind it when possible, but never the Authorization header or any token.“Never capture the Authorization header or any token.” § top
    Connectors and twins 25Identity and secrets 59
  • factEach entry is appended to a vault append lane, a write-only channel gated by a token, whose write response is exactly ok true so the capture point learns nothing.“An append lane is a write-only channel gated by a token the writer holds and nothing else” § top
    Vault design 48
  • factEach journal entry carries the hash of the one before it, so a missing, edited or reordered entry shows to anyone holding the read key.“Each entry carries the hash of the one before it” § top
    Provenance and evidence 99Vault design 48
  • factThe twin is not a copy of the mailbox; it holds only what passed through the connector, so it scales with the agent's activity rather than the mailbox's size.“The twin is not a copy of the mailbox.” § top
    Connectors and twins 25
  • factRemoved the supplier call was a delete with notifications on, so the supplier received a cancellation, and the deleted old reminder was the unpaid invoice's payment reminder, permanently deleted.“so the supplier received a cancellation” § top
    Connector Twin 2One-way door 18
  • factWith the journal every change gets a grade, and the value of the list is that the red rows, the irreversible actions, are named.“The value of the list is not that most of it is green. It is that the red rows are named.” § top
    Undo is a list, not a promiseOne-way door 18
  • factFor every change the twin holds the before and the after, so it can write the inverse call and name plainly what nobody can undo, such as a delivered email.“and name plainly the things nobody can undo, such as an email that has been delivered” § top
    Undo is a list, not a promise
  • factIdeally capture runs in a broker holding the credentials, which makes the journal complete by construction and the broker the most valuable target in the estate.“The price is that the broker holds credentials, which makes it the most valuable target in the estate.” § top
    Connectors and twins 25Identity and secrets 59twins.sgit.ai 5

Definitions (2)

  • definitionA twin, in the sense twins.sgit.ai uses, is an interface to reality rather than a simulation of it; a connector twin narrows that to the boundary between an agent and the system it may act on.“a digital twin is an interface to reality, not a simulation of it” § top
    Connectors and twins 25twins.sgit.ai 5
  • definitionRiskMandate writes an agent behaviour policy as the record of what an agent can reach, what it was authorised to do, the gap between the two, and who accepts the residual risk.“the record of what an agent can reach, what it was authorised to do, the gap between the two, and who accepts the residual risk” § top
    Behaviour policy 91Risk acceptance 37RiskMandate 97

Limits (3)

  • limitA gateway or broker capturing real Gmail and Calendar traffic, the processor and its schedule, revert execution and any customer do not exist yet.“Does not exist yet: a gateway or broker capturing real Gmail and Calendar traffic; the processor and its schedule; revert execution; any customer.” § top
    Connectors and twins 25
