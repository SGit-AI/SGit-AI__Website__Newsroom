Hypotheses (3)

  • hypothesisYou never want to charge a customer the maximum they can pay, because by definition they end up feeling it was expensive, and a customer who feels that is not happy.“You never want to charge a customer the maximum they can pay, because by definition they end up feeling it was expensive” § reader-first
    Pricing and economics 61
  • hypothesisHad the end state been designed up front it would have been over-engineered: a big project with too much attempted at once and untested features shaping other untested features.“if I had designed the end state we arrived at, I think we would have over-engineered it” § how
    Complexity and shipping 60
  • hypothesisThe real test is not the payment but whether a reader prefers the front page personalised for them, or for one of their personas, over the front page everybody gets.“It is whether a reader prefers the front page personalised for them, or for one of their personas, over the front page everybody gets.” § pieces
    Personas and personalisation 33
