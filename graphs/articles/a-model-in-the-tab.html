  • data-pointThe Prompt API accepts text, images and audio, returns text optionally constrained by a JSON schema, keeps and clones sessions, and supports five languages: English, Japanese, Spanish, German and French. 5 languages the Prompt API supports“Five languages so far: English, Japanese, Spanish, German and French.” § short
    The Prompt APIGemini 12
  • data-pointThe model needs a desktop with at least 22 GB free on the disk with the Chrome profile, and either a GPU with more than 4 GB of video memory or 16 GB of RAM and four cores. 22 GB of free disk required“At least 22 GB free on the disk with the Chrome profile.” § ships

Proposals (5)

  • proposalThe first thing worth shipping is a small honest component that shows bytes, rate and time remaining, survives a refresh, says it is safe to close the tab, and lists likely reasons when a device is unavailable.“That is the first thing worth shipping.” § download
