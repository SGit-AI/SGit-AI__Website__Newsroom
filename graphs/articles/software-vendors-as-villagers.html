Hypotheses (2)

  • hypothesisSelling the maintenance of what users build is a better revenue stream than advertising or a search deal, at a price between a pound a month and five hundred for a company.“There is a price point at which I would pay, and it is a better revenue stream than advertising or a search deal.” § maintenance
    Pricing and economics 61Explorer, villager, town planner 25
  • hypothesisBeing the villager and town planner for millions of explorers needs real engineering investment, and companies that do not make the move may be replaced from a different industry.“Some will not make that move, and their replacements may come from a completely different industry.” § settings
    Strategy and positioning 26Explorer, villager, town planner 25

Proposals (3)

  • proposalA software company should stop being the explorer that invents features and become the villager and town planner underneath users who explore on top of the product.“the company should let them explore, on top of the product, and position itself as the villager and the town planner underneath” § position
    Explorer, villager, town planner 25Strategy and positioning 26Simon Wardley 20
  • proposalLet a user tell Firefox what they want in words, let an agent make their version in a sandbox, reversibly, and charge to keep it working, per version, per change or by subscription.“Let an agent make my version, in a sandbox, reversible.” § firefox
    Firefox 9Explorer, villager, town planner 25Custom interfaces 28
  • proposalFour ways a user would pay: explore, free or a pound a month; keep, ten to fifty pounds a year; adopt, fifty to five hundred pounds a change; own, a maintained fork by contract.“Four ways a user would pay the vendor” § firefox
    Pricing and economics 61Explorer, villager, town planner 25
