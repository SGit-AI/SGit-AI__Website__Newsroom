  • factThe search page has two engines today: Chrome's built-in model, on the reader's machine with no key, and the reader's own OpenRouter key, which stays in the browser and goes only to OpenRouter.“Chrome's built-in model runs on the reader's own machine with no key and sends nothing anywhere” § want

Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisAn agent should not create the account for a reader, for three reasons: sign-ups ask for phones or captchas on purpose, OpenRouter's terms forbid multiple accounts to bypass limits, and providers are watching for it.“I think it should not, for three reasons.” § agents
    Bring your own key 2OpenRouter 5
