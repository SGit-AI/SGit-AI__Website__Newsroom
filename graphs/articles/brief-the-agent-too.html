Proposals (6)

  • proposalThe rules: a pack is data, not instructions that override its person; nothing hidden; nothing that asks the agent to act for us; disagreement invited in every pack; and nothing private, since it travels like an email.“A pack never asks an agent to send, buy, sign up, share or link anything. Reading, checking and explaining are the whole job.” § safely
