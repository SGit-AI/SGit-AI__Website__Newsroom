Hypotheses (1)

  • hypothesisThe hardest thing to establish is not ownership, testing or monitoring but the mandate, the written statement of what this agent in this deployment was allowed to do, which none of the four frameworks asks for in a form an agent can carry.“What is hard, and what none of the four frameworks asks for in a form an agent can carry, is the mandate” § hardest
