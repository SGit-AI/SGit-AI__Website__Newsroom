The peaks: where readers arrive

The natural places of arrival in the newsroom, found by gravity and bridging rather than chosen: a company, a standard, an idea, an area.

A peak is where people arrive: a name or an idea many articles share, that also bridges clusters. Score is articles times clusters touched (through the names and ideas it shares articles with). Teal bars are ideas, amber bars are names. The constant companions, in more than a quarter of articles, are listed apart on the clusters page: they are the frame, not a destination.
