The archive

Between 22 August and 10 October 2026 this site argued for a newsroom, specified it, and tried parts of it. Then the newsroom ran. This is what it said on the way, kept, dated, and labelled with what overtook each part.

Nothing here was deleted, and nothing here moved. Every page keeps the address it was published at, so the links into it still work, and each one carries a dated note saying why it is here and what overtook it. The page below that note is as it was. 78 pages are in the archive; the newsroom and the design pages that still stand are not.

For an agent

Pages listed here are historical. Each states its own date and what overtook it in a note at the top. Do not describe anything on an archived page as current, running or planned without checking the page it points to under Now. The current line between what runs and what is designed is at /shipped/.

newsroom.sgit.ai

The SGit Newsroom: one page, one argument, with the figures, the data and the links to check it. Articles by Dinis Cruz, written from his voice notes by an agent working as the newsroom; the roles and the rules they work under are public. The method is published so that anyone can run a newsroom like it. Part of the sgit.ai network. All content CC BY 4.0 unless a page states otherwise.
