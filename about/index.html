About · the purpose

Why this exists

A publication about AI agents, security and the next generation of publishing, written in the open by Dinis Cruz with a desk of agents. This page says what it is for, what it is not, and what I would like from you.

The agenda

Three things, stated plainly.

  • Share what I know, as I learn it, with the evidence attached.
  • Solve real problems in the open, so the method can be checked and copied.
  • Build sgit.ai and RiskMandate. Most of these articles use them as evidence, and I have a stake in both.
Participant disclosure: published by the sgit project, which is building the stack this site argues for. Where we lose: the full disclosure.

Open by default

Nothing here is behind a licence you have to ask about.

  • Content is CC BY 4.0: copy it, translate it, quote it, build on it, and say where it came from.
  • Code is Apache 2.0.
  • Every idea here is free to take, adapt and build on.

How it is written

The words start as my voice notes.

  • A desk of named agents turns the notes into articles, under rules that are published.
  • Every version is kept, so you can see what changed and when.
  • The method is published so anyone can run a newsroom like it. It lives in the workshop, not on the front page.

Who it is for

Each persona is a front page made for one kind of reader. Pick the one closest to you.

  • I am building a company: Building a company: what to build, what to charge, what to secure and when to stop. Strategy, pricing and the questions investors ask.
  • I work in news: Where news came from and where it could go: story vaults, liquid content, local evidence, and what publishers are owed by the machines that read them.
  • I run security: Agents as insiders: who you are protecting against, blast radius, identity, incidents and the controls that hold when a model does not.
  • I build agents: Making agents work day to day: Claude Code sessions, hooks, memory, behaviour policies, and the engineering lessons each mistake left behind.
  • I sit on a board: How AI is changing the business and how to govern it: business models, due diligence, risk acceptance, accountability and the governance frameworks.
  • I publish news: What a reader gets, what the newsroom keeps, what it costs to adopt, and the code that runs it, as an API.

What I want from you

Four things, in rising order of commitment.

  1. Read. Start with your persona's first article.
  2. Rate. Tell me whether a piece was useful. One tap, with your consent.
  3. Tell me what is missing. The article you wanted and did not find.
  4. Put your agents on a problem. If a problem here is yours, ask about putting your agents on it.

The heart line of the front page is Pay for what you read.: The reading meter charges a few pence a page from credit kept in your browser, by how far you read and how useful you said it was. Nothing to sign up for.
