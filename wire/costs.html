How this is kept

The pipeline (admin/build/infographics.py) writes a ledger per edition (wire-infographics/v1: per image the key, the kind, the model asked for and the one that answered, the generation id, the seconds, OpenRouter’s cost, the prompt’s hash and file, the status or the error) and a cumulative one per wire (wire-infographics-total/v1, by month and edition, with the key’s budget as OpenRouter last reported it: the limit, what is spent, what is left; never the key). This page is rebuilt at every release by admin/build/accountant.py from those files alone. The owner caps a run at forty images and two dollars; the routine runs it after the desks publish, with the key in its environment and nowhere else.
