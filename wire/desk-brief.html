For the desks: the brief from the newsroom

What the newsroom asks of the desks that write the vaults, dated, newest first; a desk reads it at the start of every run.

The wires: The Industry WireThe AWS WireThe OWASP WireThe Portugal Wire · Day by dayWhat it costsThe charterFor the desks

A standing brief from newsroom.sgit.ai (the side that reads the wires' vaults) to the desks that write them: the Industry Wire desk, the OWASP Wire desk, the AWS Wire desk, and any desk added under the same contract. The owner passes items here; the desk reads this file at the start of every run (https://newsroom.sgit.ai/wire/desk-brief.md), newest first. Nothing in it is a secret: a desk holds its vault's write key, this site holds only read keys, and the read keys are published. The rules every item follows are the charter.

2026-10-13: from the owner's voice memo, relayed by the newsroom

16 October: one wording for the four desks, and Sonnet. The AWS and Portugal runs were refused at the clone and the install this morning while the Industry run got through on a retry. Your four prompts now share one wording (what the run is and who authorised it, the clone key in the owner's form, install and clone as separate commands) and run on Sonnet, with the verifier on Haiku. End every report with one line saying whether the permission check refused a command, and whether a retry went through: the accountant records it. Your runs' cost and tokens are now on what the wires cost, per run.

Two wires about people. The owner asked for two more wires: the Dinis Cruz Wire (his own public work, at the level of a release note) and the rUv Wire (the public work of Reuven Cohen and the Agentics Foundation). Their vaults hold a brief and docs/PERSON-WIRE-RULES.md, now also in the charter: works, not life; the person's own publication as the primary; nothing behind a login; corrections and stopping; agreement recorded. Each first run builds its pipeline from the Industry Wire's.

The first unattended runs, 14 October. None of the four scheduled runs changed a vault this morning, and the newsroom's own routine did not run either: the scheduled tasks' permission check refused the clone of a vault with a key and the install of the sgit tool, so each session stopped before its first step. Nothing is wrong with the vaults or the contract. The owner is asked to allow those two commands in the tasks' settings or to pre-install sgit in the environment; until then, publish by hand as before and the site picks it up.

The edition is now drawn as graphs, and since v0.12.59 as pictures. From v0.12.42 every wire page draws, from the bundle alone, a provenance strip under each headline and, folded in the header, the stories per day by beat, the threads on a date axis and the entities two or more stories name. Nothing new is needed from you: a date on every story, a beat, coverage[] with source ids from the registry, and entities where you have them, as the contract already says. The newsroom, not the desks, makes an infographic per story and per edition on its side, from the fields the contract has (the first live editions of 14 October are on the pages, each with its model, its cost and its prompt); three optional fields would make them better: card.claim (a claim of at most eight words per story, for the card; derived from the headline until then), brief.personal.article_ready[] on the Portugal Wire, and _pt twins of editor_note and lead.why_it_leads.

Run on a schedule. The owner asks for the wires to run every day at seven in the morning (London), each desk in its own session, each run ending in one commit with a release entry. The newsroom runs its own morning check after that (a routine on this site: clone, compare, refresh the snapshots, release when something changed), so a desk that publishes by seven is on the site by eight. Weekly wires publish on their day and say so in wire.cadence.

Read the reader's lane. Both your release note and the personal brief of 13 October said the reader's lane was not read. The runbook's step 1 reads it when the agent has been given the lane's read key; the owner holds that key and hands it over outside any vault. Once you have it: a wrong becomes a correction, the rest become proposed edits to data/personas.json, as a diff for approval, never applied silently.

A verifier on every run, every story. The charter asks for a third round: an independent agent, on a different model where possible, that takes the vault as published and checks every story: the quote verbatim in its source, the date, the version, whether the coverage sources are independent or copies of one release, and whether the summary claims anything the sources do not say. Write what it found to data/factcheck-<date>.json as wire-factcheck/v1:

{
  "schema": "wire-factcheck/v1",
  "checked_by": "independent verifier (model name), 2026-10-14",
  "method": "fetched quote_source_url and primary for every story; checked coverage independence",
  "results": [
    {"story": "ow-09", "quote": "exact", "date": "ok", "version": "ok", "independence": "3 groups", "verdict": "ok", "fix": null},
    {"story": "ow-07", "quote": "exact", "date": "ok", "version": "n/a", "independence": "1 group", "verdict": "fixed", "fix": "moderator claim removed; town hall time conflict noted"}
  ],
  "not_checked": "none"
}

The site draws each results[] row at its story, and the file's checked_by, method and not_checked in the edition's header. A fix is applied by the desk as a dated entry in the story's changes[], in the same commit. The first two editions' checks (a sample of stories, a prose result) are shown as they are; from the next run, one row per story.

One list per wire. Readers can now subscribe to one wire alone, from each wire's page. The request goes down the same subscribe lane as the newsletter, encrypted to the same recipient, with two headers to tell it apart: X-SGit-Form: subscribe as before, and X-SGit-List: wire:industry, wire:owasp or wire:aws (the subject says the wire's name). The agent that drains the lane keeps one list per value; a request without the header is the newsletter, as before. When a desk publishes, the personal-newsletter agents can send that wire's release note to that wire's list.

A Portugal wire. The owner asks for one more wire: the same beats as the Industry Wire (building with AI and models, AI business, open source, cybersecurity, risk, governance and regulation), for Portugal: Portuguese sources and Portuguese stories, in Portuguese and English, under the same contract, with wire saying {"id": "portugal", "title": "The Portugal Wire"}. It feeds the Portuguese publication this site designed (pt.newsroom.sgit.ai) and the Portugal Startups beat already in the archive. The persona dinis is the named reader, by the same agreement. Build the vault, publish the read key to the owner, and the newsroom adds it with one config file.

Who should know. Each wire now has a reach page listing, by organisation route only, who a story concerns and how to reach them, so that the owner's team can tell them the wire exists and send them what it found. Add to the vault, when you can, data/contacts.json under this site's published contact rules (organisations, not people; a role address from the organisation's own contact page, one hop; the page frozen and hashed; a failure is a result). The newsroom draws it beside what it found itself. For the OWASP Wire in particular: the licence finding of ow-09 (an "All rights reserved" footer on the new owasp.org against the Foundation's own CC BY-SA licence) is worth putting to the Foundation directly, by its published support route, as a question, not a verdict.

What happened, day by day. The site now shows every wire day by day: the releases published that day and the stories dated that day, by source. It needs nothing new from you beyond what the contract has: a date on every story and an at on every release.

The release note, every publish. Done since 13 October on all three wires. Keep it: one entry per publish, newest first, the commit message starting with the release id.

For an agent

The standing brief for the desks that write the wires' vaults, read at the start of every run from https://newsroom.sgit.ai/wire/desk-brief.md: run at seven each morning (London); read the reader's lane once given its read key; a verifier on every run writing wire-factcheck/v1 to data/factcheck-.json with one row per story; one subscription list per wire (X-SGit-List: wire: on the subscribe lane); a Portugal wire under the same contract; data/contacts.json under the published contact rules; a date on every story and an at on every release; a release note on every publish. The markdown is the record.
